Contract scaling plans (funded accounts only)
Scaling applies 
only to funded accounts
. There are no scaling restrictions during the evaluation phase, where you can use your full contract allocation from day one.
How scaling works
Once your account is funded, your maximum contract size increases as your account grows. Your allowed contract size is determined by your 
end-of-day (EOD) closing balance
 and updates once per day.

Important clarifications on scaling
Scaling applies per individual account
Contract limits do not combine across multiple accounts
Scaling is based on simulated profit only
Limits are determined using your end-of-day balance
Scaling does not override other risk rules, such as the Daily Loss Limit or the Trailing Drawdown
What does "max contracts" mean?
The "max contracts" rule ensures you trade within safe limits for your account. 
Minis
 are standard futures contracts, while 
micros
 are one-tenth the size of minis. You can trade minis, micros, or a combination of both, as long as you stay within your limit.
Example:
 on a $50,000 Elite ACCESS funded account at full allocation, you can trade up to 3 minis, 30 micros, or a combination such as 2 minis and 10 micros.
Always check the table for your specific account type. Contract limits are not the same across all programs: a $50,000 S2F Sim Pro account allows 4 minis, while a $50,000 Elite or Instant Sim Funded account allows 3 minis. Staying within these limits helps manage risk and keep your account compliant. It is the trader's personal responsibility to monitor and follow the clearly outlined contract scaling rules.
Exceeding your contract limit
Exceeding your allowed contract limit is considered a 
rule violation
. If the contract scaling rules are not followed:
Trades may be rejected or reduced
The account may be flagged for review
Profits generated while exceeding contract limits may be removed
Payout requests may be deemed ineligible
Compliance with scaling rules is 
mandatory for payout eligibility
.
