End of Day (EOD) Rules
Fundamental
Premium
During the evaluation phase, we carefully monitor your performance and apply risk management mechanisms to help you succeed as a trader.
Authorized Drawdown Management
Your authorized drawdown is calculated at the end of each trading day, and this calculation does not change during the day. However, if your balance reaches and exceeds the value of your drawdown limit, you will be liquidated. When your account balance reaches a new profit peak at the end of the trading day, the drawdown is calculated taking into account only the profits realized at that time, excluding unrealized profits.
Trailing End of Day
Trailing end of day involves automatically adjusting the daily stop-loss level as your capital moves upward. The daily stop-loss level is adjusted at the end of each trading session, which ends at 
10:00 PM UTC+2
.
50K Eval Drawdown
$2,500 max loss
100K Eval Drawdown
$3,000 max loss
50K Funded Floor
$50,100 (trailing stops here)
100K Funded Floor
$100,100 (trailing stops here)
Session End
10:00 PM UTC+2
How It Works — Example
If your starting capital is $50,000, you have a stop-loss of $2,500 for the day. You cannot go below $47,500 or you will be liquidated. If you end the day at +$500 profit, your stop-loss will be automatically adjusted to -$2,500 against your new balance at the end of the day, bringing your liquidation price to $48,000 instead of $47,500.
On evaluation accounts:
 The EOD Drawdown always persists until the end of the evaluation.
On funded accounts:
 Your EOD Drawdown stops at $50,100 for a 50K account and at $100,100 for a 100K account. Once the EOD Drawdown reaches these limits, it will no longer trail.
Important:
 Your liquidation price remains fixed for the day. Regardless of the gains made during the day, you will only be liquidated when your capital reaches the day's limit. For example, if you start with $50,000 and your liquidation value is $47,500, even if you make $1,000 in the morning, your EOD stop loss remains at $47,500 — allowing a maximum loss of $3,500 for the rest of that day.
Note — Express to Live accounts:
 E2L accounts use a 
static drawdown
, not EOD trailing. The EOD rules described above do not apply to E2L accounts. See the Express to Live Account Rules section for details.

Consistency Policy
Fundamental
Premium
At Phidias Propfirm, we attach great importance to discipline, consistency and security in our trading operations.
1. Defined Trading System
We are looking for traders who have developed a precise and well-defined trading system. This system must include clear rules for entries, stops, profit taking, drawdown management and exits.
2. Consistency of Operations
Traders must maintain consistency in their position sizes, number of trading days, and risk management. DCA (Dollar Cost Averaging) is authorized to smooth entry points. The results attributed to the Trader are calculated on the basis of trades actually executed and entered into the market.
3. No Automated / HFT Trading
The use of robots, fully automated trading algorithms, or any other form of automated trading is not permitted. Only semi-automated software is permitted, provided the trader actively monitors and manually adjusts all trades.
4. No Lottery Transactions
We strongly discourage seeking exceptional trades or attempting to accumulate funds quickly by taking excessive risks. We do not support "one-time withdrawal" strategies. Example: inverse operation (buy vs sell) via multiple accounts on the same asset. Full margin trading during high volatility economic announcements.
5. News Trading
Economic news trading is permitted on all account types.
6. Respect & Professionalism
Traders must treat our staff, partners and other traders with respect and professionalism at all times.
7. Profit Consistency
For CASH accounts only: under no circumstances should the profit balance from a single trading day exceed 30% of the total balance. See 
30% Consistency Rule
 for details.
8. Contract Expiration
You are required to trade the highest volume and most recent contract for the instrument you wish to trade. Trading in illiquid, low-volume contracts is not permitted. Spread trading between two maturities on the same product is forbidden.
9. Treasury Bonds
Arbitrage strategies on Treasury bonds for one tick cannot be replicated in real conditions. This practice is prohibited on evaluation and funded accounts. Any trader wishing to use Treasury bonds must contact us to activate the necessary market data.
Note — Express to Live accounts:
 The Profit Consistency Policy does not apply to Express to Live accounts (25K, 50K, 100K, 150K). E2L accounts have no profit consistency rule and no daily loss limit.
11
30% Consistency Rule
Fundamental
Premium
Applies To
CASH accounts only
Rule
No single day can exceed 30% of total profit
Min. Calculation Period
4 trading days
How It Works
If your total profit is $1,000 on your account, one of the previous days cannot exceed $300 (30% of $1,000 = $300). But if it does, no worries — you just need to increase your total profit. For example, if you make $330 on your best day, you just need to increase your total profit from $1,000 to $1,100 and you enter the consistency rule (30% of $1,100 = $330).
Example
If you make $1,000 in 4 days ($250 each day), your best profit day is $250, and $250 out of $1,000 total profit represents 25% — you pass.
But if, out of those 4 days, you have one day at $350, one day at $150 and two other days at $250, it is still $1,000. However, your best profit day is $350. That is 35% of $1,000 total profit, exceeding the 30% rule.
Note — Express to Live accounts:
 The 30% Consistency Rule does not apply to Express to Live accounts (25K, 50K, 100K, 150K). E2L accounts have no consistency rule.
