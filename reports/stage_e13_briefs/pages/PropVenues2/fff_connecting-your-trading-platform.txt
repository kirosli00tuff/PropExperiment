Connecting Your Trading Platform
How to connect your preferred trading platform
August 11, 2026
Table of contents
Connecting to NinjaTrader
Log in to the NinjaTrader website using your FFF credentials. 
Download and install the NinjaTrader desktop application. 
Open NinjaTrader and sign in with the same FFF username and password.
Select 
Simulation
 and click 
Launch. 
Connecting to Tradovate
Choose one of the following:
Web:
 app.tradovate.com
Mobile:
 Download the Tradovate app for iOS or Android.
Desktop:
 Download the Tradovate desktop application from the Tradovate website.
Log In
Sign in using the username and password provided in your FFF Dashboard. 
Select the correct server or workspace for your account type (Evaluation or Funded).
If prompted, allow the required trading permissions.
Connecting to TradingView
Sign the Tradovate Agreement.
 Log in to Tradovate with your FFF credentials and complete the required agreement when prompted.
Enable the TradingView Add On.
 In Tradovate, navigate to 
Settings → Add Ons
 and enable the 
TradingView Add On
.
Log in to TradingView and open any chart. 
Click Trading Panel, select Tradovate, then sign in using your 
FFF dashboard credentials.
Select the 
Demo
 environment, then approve the connection and any requested permissions. 
Connecting to WealthCharts
Use WealthCharts on either of the following platforms:
Web:
https://www.wealthcharts.com/
Mobile:
 iOS or Android (search 
"WealthCharts"
)
After purchasing your account, you will receive 
temporary login credentials
. From your 
FFF Dashboard
, navigate to 
Trading Platforms
 and launch 
WealthCharts
.
Sign in using your 
temporary login credentials
. After your first login, you can change your password at any time.
When prompted to connect a broker, select 
Funded Futures Family
 to access your trading accounts.
Once connected, your accounts will be available in WealthCharts. For the best experience, avoid logging in on multiple devices at the same time.
