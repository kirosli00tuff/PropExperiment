Can I Switch My Trading Platform (Tradovate / NinjaTrader or BlackArrow)?
Why the trading platform you pick at checkout (Tradovate / NinjaTrader or BlackArrow) is permanent for that account, and how to trade on another platform.
Updated yesterday
Table of contents
When purchasing an account with Top One Futures, you can select your preferred 
trading platform
, such as 
Tradovate / NinjaTrader
 or 
BlackArrow
. Please review our platform selection policy carefully before completing your purchase, because the choice is 
permanent
 for that account.
Permanent platform selection policy
Once an account is generated on a specific platform, platform switches are 
strictly not permitted
. The platform chosen during checkout remains permanently assigned to that specific account for its entire lifecycle.
Key policy guidelines
Situation
Can I switch platform?
Active, paused, or inactive account
No. You cannot transfer an account from one platform to another mid-evaluation
Passed evaluation / funded account
No. Your new funded account is automatically issued on the exact same platform used during the evaluation
Account reset
No. A reset restores your account balance and rules, but the reset account remains on its original platform
How to trade on a different platform
If you would like to trade using a different platform (for example, moving from Tradovate/NinjaTrader to BlackArrow, or vice versa), you must select your desired platform when 
purchasing a new account
 at checkout.
Important reminder:
 please double-check your platform choice before completing your order, as account setups are automated and platform selections cannot be modified once generated.
