Contract Rollover

Contract Rollover

TopstepX auto-updates every futures product to the current front month — no need to track rollover dates or volume yourself.

Contracts are automatically rolled over during the daily market close window (4:00 PM – 5:00 PM CST) based on an internal algorithm to determine the optimal time to roll active contracts prior to the next trading day based on volume, open interest, and expiration dates.

Trade Copier

Trade Copier

The Trade Copier lets you mirror trades from a Lead account to 1 or more Follower accounts automatically — so you can run the same strategy across multiple accounts without placing every order manually.

  • Lead account — where you place your trades

  • Follower account(s) — where those trades are copied to

✋ Before you connect accounts, please know:

  • The Lead account must have the lowest Maximum Position Size (MPS) of all accounts in the group. Running a 50K, 100K, and 150K? The 50K is your Lead.

  • If your Lead and Follower accounts are at different Scaling Plan tiers — meaning their margin requirements differ — the Trade Copier may disconnect automatically at the start of a new trading day. Always verify your copy trading settings at the start of each session.

  • Trade copying is only available for Trading Combine® and Express Funded Account® accounts. Live Funded Account® accounts cannot use the Trade Copier.

  • Filled orders on Follower accounts only appear in the Order Log after they're filled — not when placed.

  • While a Follower account is connected, you can't trade it directly. If the order buttons are greyed out, the account is still linked. Remove it in Settings to trade it independently.

  • Executions are mirrored — but exact fill price and timing may vary slightly due to slippage, liquidity, and market conditions.

  • Turning off the Trade Copier mid-trade will immediately flatten all Follower accounts.

Linking / Unlinking

  • If the Lead hits its Daily Loss Limit or is auto-liquidated, all Follower accounts are flattened with it and no further trades copy while the Lead is locked out. The accounts stay linked — copying resumes when the Lead can trade again. You may unlink the accounts manually to trade the followers separately.

  • If the Lead hits its Maximum Loss Limit, the account is permanently violated; unlink the Followers in Settings to trade them independently.

  • If you select Liquidate Only when setting your PDLL/PDPT, when of these violations fires on a follower, that account does get dropped from the copier before it's flattened.

  • If you select Liquidate and Block when setting your PDLL/PDPT, when one of these violations fires on a follower, the account is locked out. It auto-resumes the next trading and does not get removed from Trade Copier.

  • If the Scaling plan does not match between the Leader and followers - follower account will be automatically removed at the start of the trading day

  • When a Payout request is submitted, Follower accounts are automatically unlinked. Check your settings at the start of every session.

⚠️ Important: Please read more about how copy trading can affect potential for hedging here.

  4. Save

Auto-OCO Brackets

Auto-OCO Brackets

Every entry gets its own take-profit and stop-loss — no manual placement needed. (OCO = One Cancels Other)

  • When one fills, the other cancels automatically

  • Brackets apply per entry, not across the full position

  • Must be flat to enable or switch bracket types

  • Using the DOM? OCO must also be enabled there.

Setup: Settings → Risk Settings → Switch to Auto-OCO → set values → Save.
​
Enable Auto-OCO brackets directly on the specific interface(s) you trade from, whether that is the Chart, Order Panel, DOM, or a combination of them.

👉 Important: If you use ticks, TopstepX requires you to enter a value greater than 5 ticks. If you use a dollar value, TopstepX will reject your orders if the dollar value entered does not place your brackets at least 4 ticks away from the entry price.

NEW: Range Bars

Range Bars

👉 Let price — not the clock — decide when a bar prints, so trends, consolidation, and breakouts stand out without the noise of time-based charts.

What it shows

Each Range Bar represents a fixed price move instead of a fixed slice of time. A new bar prints only when price travels your chosen range (say, 10 ticks) — no move, no new bar, no matter how much time passes. Quiet stretches compress into a few bars; strong moves stretch into a long run of them.

How do I add Range Bars? Range Bars live inside the Advanced Charts (Beta) component. Press the plus sign to add a component, then drag Advanced Charts (Beta) into your layout.

What you'll see

  • Long run of same-color bars — a real trend, completing bars quickly.

  • Alternating bars in a tight zone — consolidation. Price isn't committing to a direction.

  • Bars printing faster — volatility is rising, common around news or the session open.

  • Bars slowing down — price has stalled.

  • A clean break from a tight cluster — a shift from balance to directional bars.

  • Trendlines, channels, and support/resistance tend to line up more consistently than on a time chart.

Where it fits

Range Bars work on any futures contract, on intraday charts. Range size is adjustable per instrument, since a fixed tick count behaves differently across contracts.

👉 Please note: Range Bars have been launched in Beta as a real-time chart type. Bars build forward from when you open the chart — you can't scroll back to bars that printed before, and reloading or switching symbols starts the build fresh. Historical data (rebuilding past bars from tick history) is planned for a future release.

Range Bars FAQs


​Why does my data reset?
Beta data resets daily, on top of any reload, symbol switch, or connection drop.

Can I customize my settings?
Yes. At launch you can choose from 4 chart types and 7 ranges (plus a custom range), and you can change bar color, line color, and timezone.

Can I link my chart?
Yes, using the link control in the top-left corner of the component.

Can I add indicators to Advanced Charts?

Not at this time. Because it's a custom chart, a number of things aren't available yet, including indicators, drawing tools, and right-click to place chart orders.

How do I sync the crosshairs from my main chart?

You can't. Crosshair syncing between your main chart and Advanced Charts isn't supported.

Contract Limits

Contract Limits

Cap the number of contracts you can trade per symbol.

  • Orders exceeding the limit are automatically rejected

  • Applies to order entry only — won't close open positions

  • Take Profit and Stop Loss bracket orders are ignored

  • Enforced separately for long and short

Setup: Settings → Risk Settings → Contract Limits → enter limits → Save.
​
​This is also where you can see current contract limits according to your account type/size.
​

Trade Limits

Trade Limits

Cap total trades per day and/or week.

  • Open positions can still be managed

  • Every entry and exit counts as a trade

  • Once the limit is hit, no new trades

  • Trade limits do NOT auto-close open positions

Setup: Settings → Risk Settings → Trade Limits → enter max daily/weekly → Save.

Trade Limits allow you to set a maximum number of trades you can take per day and/or week.

⚠️ Important: Any pending orders placed prior to hitting the limit must be manually cancelled. TopstepX will not automatically remove pre-existing pending orders upon a trade limit lockout.

Daily Risk Lock

Daily Risk Lock

Lock your risk settings for the day. Covers PDLL, PDPT, and Trade Limits.

  • Can't be changed until the next trading day

  • Unlocks at 5 PM CT

  • Support cannot reverse a lock once applied

Setup: Settings → Risk Settings → set limits → Save → Lock Risk Settings for Day → Confirm.

Lockout

Lockout

Manually block yourself from trading for a set period. Per account — must be set separately for each.

  • Choose a session (NY, London, etc.) or custom duration (15 min, 1 hr, all day)

  • Once applied, it can't be canceled

  • All open positions and working orders close immediately

  • Support cannot reverse a lockout

Setup: Settings → Risk Settings → Set Lockout → choose option.

Personal Daily Profit Target/Daily Loss Limit

Personal Daily Profit Target/Daily Loss Limit

The Personal Daily Profit Target (PDPT) and Personal Daily Loss Limit (PDLL) are optional risk settings in TopstepX that allow you to define daily profit and loss thresholds. Learn more about the PDLL here.

Setup: Settings → Risk Settings → enter limits → choose action → Save.

When a threshold is hit, your account responds based on the action you selected. Final outcome depends on market volatility, slippage, and contracts being exited at the time.

Trailing Personal Daily Loss Limit (TPDLL)

The TPDLL is an advanced risk management tool that allows Traders to set a loss limit that dynamically adjusts based on their highest reached account balance. Once the limit trails up, it never moves back down—locking in gains and managing your risk in real time. Learn more about the TPDLL here.

General Troubleshooting

If you’re experiencing latency or slow performance:

  • Lower your Data Speed (Slow or Medium Recommended)

  • Remove indicators and add them back one at a time

  • Reduce the number of active indicators

  • If you’re on mobile, try using another device such as a laptop or desktop

  • Try another browser or incognito mode

  • Clear your Cache and Cookies

  • Use a website to check your connection quality (not speed test) to USA based servers.

⚡️ TopstepX uses real-time exchange data with no artificial delay. On slower machines or Wi-Fi connections, this may impact performance.

Data Feed & Connection

Data Feed & Connection

TopstepX pulls data straight from the exchange(s)— no third-party feed. Data servers sit in Northern Virginia, and every Trader connects there regardless of location for market data.

Pick your refresh speed:

  • Slow — 750ms

  • Medium — 250ms (default)

  • Fast — 50ms

Chart lag does not affect trading and orders — order execution runs server-side, not through your browser. Traders are advised to monitor their Positions tab at all times.

Mobile Devices

TopstepX is mobile-friendly and can be accessed through your phone’s web browser, allowing you to manage trades and monitor the market on the go. Most desktop features are available on mobile, providing a consistent and flexible trading experience.

For the best experience, use a smartphone, and tablets may perform better in desktop mode. Please note there is currently no mobile app. If the mobile version is not loading correctly, ensure that “Desktop Mode” is disabled in your browser settings.

Mobile Navigation

Account Info—View a snapshot of your account balance, UP&L, RP&L, and parameters. Switch between accounts via the dropdown or trigger a Lockout right from this panel — once enabled, it can't be reversed.

All Accounts—Overview of every account tied to your login, including status (Active, Temp. Liquidation, Goal Achieved, or Ineligible), Created On date, Account Name, Win Rate, Total Lots Traded, Day P&L, Open P&L, and Account Balance.

Positions—Monitor open positions in with details on Time, Symbol, Position size, Entry Price, Risk (Stop Loss), To Make (Take Profit), P&L, and a 1-tap Close button.

Orders—Track open, filled, canceled, or rejected orders with key price levels and a quick Cancel option.

Trades—Review your completed round-trip trade history, including Symbol, Size, Time, Entry Price, Exit Price, P&L, and Fees.

Mobile DOM:

  • Limit Orders — tap Bid Size (buy, below market) or Ask Size (sell, above market)

  • Stop Market Orders — tap Bid Size (buy, above market) or Ask Size (sell, below market)

  • Breakeven ("B/E") Order — appears once a position shows a positive unrealized P&L. Tap it to place a Stop Market order at your average entry price

Mobile Charts:

  • Change symbol — tap the "" near the chart's right edge and pick from the dropdown

  • TP/SL from chart — tap your position indicator, then drag up or down. A blue-outlined dot shows it's active to move

  • Order types on mobile charts — Market, Limit, Stop, and Trailing Stop, all accessible from the same "" button

🛜 Important: Because mobile performance depends on your device, Wi-Fi, and data provider, we’re unable to troubleshoot mobile-specific issues that may be related to connectivity.
