Customizing your charting experience with TD Active Trader gives you quicker access to the information you need. You can set up different layouts for specific strategies or based on how many screens or monitors you have.
Start customizing by choosing the chart range and interval. You can do this in a couple of ways. First, you can set the range by selecting one of the presets at the bottom of the chart. If you're looking for something more specific, click the plus icon to customize it. From there you can adjust the interval as well.
To zoom in or out on a chart, use your mouse scroll wheel, the plus or minus magnifying glass, or the slide bar at the bottom of the chart. The chart defaults to candlesticks, but you can click the candle icon to choose from a number of different chart types.
And if one chart just isn't enough, you can add more. Add an additional chart tab to the widget by clicking the plus sign. Or click the layout button to add multiple charts to the same tab. From there, you can also choose which features you want to synchronize between charts.
The gear icon gives you access to a number of additional settings. In the general section, you can toggle on or off different features, like highs and lows, gridlines, crosshairs, and more. Some of these will display on the chart, while others will display below it.
In the trading section, you can choose to display order positions and alerts on your chart. Next, you can choose which items appear in the legend. And in the "Scales" section, you can customize the scale of the right axis as well as how it's labelled.
Let's be honest, colours are a big part of what makes charts so helpful to traders. That's why we give you the ability to customize the colour of each aspect of the chart.
Keeping an eye on the markets isn't always about trading prices. In the "Events" section, you can add corporate events to your chart or remove them. Need a little more room to see how a study or drawing might resolve? Under the "Expansion" section, you can add or remove blank spaces on the top, right, or bottom of the chart.
Once you have everything set up the way you like, be sure to save the layout using the layout menu at the top right of the platform.

Widgets are modules in TD Active Trader that serve a specific purpose. For example, the "Chart" widget lets you view a chart… The "Watchlist" widget lets you view a watchlist… And so on. They're like the tabs on your internet browser, except they can be arranged in many different ways on your screen.
There are two common ways to add a widget. First, you can choose a widget from a categorized list at the top of the platform. It will be added somewhere to the existing workspace based on the type of widget you chose. You can also drag the widget from the menu. While still holding the mouse button down, hover over an area of the platform to see where the widget will go. Release the mouse button to add the widget to your desired location.
You can also add a widget as an additional tab by clicking the "plus" sign at the top of a widget that's already on display. Then, simply select the widget you want from the list.
Widgets can also be rearranged after they've been added. Left-click the six-dot icon of the widget you want to move, and then drag your mouse to the new location. Widgets will also have some icons in the top-right corner. Some widgets will have a hamburger menu that lets you further customize it. The most common feature found here is column customization. The other icons are very similar to any internet browser.
Clicking the "x" icon will delete a widget or close a detached window. Just make sure you're clicking the right "x" so you don't close the platform browser. Click the overlapping squares icon to detach the widget from the main platform. This is ideal for multiple or ultrawide monitor setups. Once you've detached a widget, you'll see a minus sign. Click it to minimize the detached widget.

There are many ways to start a multi-leg option trade in TD Active Trader. One way is to click the bid or ask price of the option you want to trade. Then click the plus sign beside a bid or ask of another option to add it to the ticket. Alternatively, while holding down the control key on your keyboard, click the bid or ask price of any options you want to add to your multi-leg order.
You can also set the Option Chain to show quotes for a variety of strategies. Select a strategy from the dropdown menu in the upper left of the option chain. Then enter the width between the strike prices in the "St Width" field. Launch a trade ticket by left-clicking the bid or ask price. You can also right-click an existing position, order, or quote to start a trade ticket. This is especially helpful when rolling a position—closing an existing option position and opening a new one within the same order.
To start a multi-leg option trade from the Positions widget, manually highlight multiple options by left-clicking them while holding down the control key. Then right-click to start a trade ticket from the menu.
In the first section of the ticket, you'll see the name of the strategy you selected, or the word "Custom" if you're using a non-standard strategy. So that your order remains valid based on the strategy you selected, you won't be able to change certain parameters of the ticket. For example, for a vertical spread, one leg must be a buy and the other a sell… The number of contracts and expiration dates must be the same… And both legs must be calls or puts.
However, if you want complete control to customize your order, click the lock icon to make changes. When you do, the Spread field may change to Custom. Everything else on the ticket is the same as with a single-leg order.
Keep in mind that your order duration can't be longer than the earliest expiration date in your trade. Once you've updated your order, click Confirm and Send. Review the order confirmation screen, and if something isn't correct, click Go Back to make changes. When everything is to your liking, click Send to place the trade.
After you place your trade, head over to the Orders widget to check on the status.
