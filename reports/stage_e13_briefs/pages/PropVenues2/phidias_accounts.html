01Which account type is right for me?
It depends on your trading style and goals.If you want the fastest path to a LIVE funded account with fixed risk, choose Express to Live. If you day-trade and need larger capital, go Fundamental. If you want the most flexible rules with overnight holds, payouts every 5 days, a progressive split, and a Cash account reset, pick Premium. If you want to compete for cash prizes with low entry cost, try the 10K Challenge.
02What is the difference between static and EOD drawdown?
Static never moves. EOD updates at close. Both are dramatically fairer than intraday trailing.Static drawdown (Express to Live accounts) is a fixed dollar amount that never changes regardless of your profits. EOD drawdown (Fundamental and Premium) is recalculated only at market close. Neither trails your intraday profits like most competitors. A $500 win followed by a $600 pullback can breach your account at a trailing firm. At Phidias, that same scenario leaves your account intact.
03Can I switch account types later?
You can run multiple account types simultaneously.You can have up to 15 funded accounts at the same time, mixing Express to Live, Fundamental, and Premium accounts. Many traders start with a 25K Express to Live to test the waters, then add Fundamental or Premium accounts as they scale.
04What is the difference between one-time payment and monthly?
One-time payment means no monthly fees and $0 activation fee.Monthly subscription costs less upfront but you pay each month until you cancel. When you pass, there is an activation fee to start your funded account. One-time payment is a single charge with no activation fee and no recurring billing. Most traders choose one-time payment because the total cost is lower.
05What happens when I pass my evaluation?
You get a funded Cash account. Then you trade toward a LIVE account.After passing, your evaluation becomes a funded Cash account with the same rules. For Express to Live accounts, hit the profit target again to earn a bonus payout (80/20 split) plus a live trading credit, and upgrade to a LIVE account with Dorman Trading from your very first payout. For Fundamental and Premium accounts, the live transition is strictly discretionary and reviewed by the Phidias risk team from $100K in cumulative profits across all accounts.
06How fast are payouts processed?
90% of payouts processed under 30 minutes. Always within 24 hours.Payouts are processed to your Phidias Wallet, where you can withdraw to your bank or reinvest into a new evaluation instantly. With the 25K Express to Live, the entire path from signup to first payout can happen in 48 hours.
07Have you ever denied a payout?
No. Zero payouts denied in Phidias history. Ever.Our rules are objective, not subjective. There is no judgment call that can block your payout. You either met the criteria or you did not. Everything is on one dedicated rules page, visible before you buy.
08Can I hold positions overnight?
Yes, with Premium accounts. Our data shows swing traders have an 83% higher success rate.Premium accounts (50K, 100K, 150K) let you hold positions overnight and over the weekend. This is based on our data from 25,000+ traders. Most prop firms force close before the bell. If your strategy needs room to breathe, Premium is built for you.
09How many funded accounts can I have?
Up to 15 funded accounts simultaneously.You can run up to 15 funded accounts at the same time, combining any mix of Express to Live, Fundamental, and Premium accounts. Use the Phidias Wallet to reinvest payouts into new evaluations instantly.
10What is the path to a LIVE account?
Real capital through Dorman Trading. The goal every prop firm trader should aim for.LIVE accounts trade real capital through Dorman Trading, a registered FCM. For Express to Live: pass evaluation, then hit your first profit target on the Cash account to upgrade straight to LIVE. For Fundamental and Premium: the live transition is strictly discretionary and reviewed by the Phidias risk team from $100K in cumulative profits across all accounts. LIVE accounts get daily unlimited payouts with no ceiling.
11What is the profit split on Phidias accounts?
Up to 100% profit split on Premium accounts. Most prop firms cap traders at 80% or 90%.Express to Live and Fundamental accounts use a fixed 80/20 profit split (you keep 80%). Premium accounts use a progressive split that climbs from 75% to 100% as you stack payouts: 75% on payout 1, 80% on payout 2, 85% on payout 3, 90% on payout 4, and 100% from payout 5 onwards. After 5 payouts on a Premium account, you keep every dollar of profit you generate.
