FAQ
Common questions
What markets can I trade with GFF Brokers? GFF Brokers provides access to futures, options on futures, and forex markets. You can trade popular products like E-mini S&P 500, Micro E-minis, crude oil, gold, currencies, and many more across global exchanges.
What is the minimum account size to start trading? You can open an account with as little as $250. Day trading margins on micro e-mini products start from just $50 per contract, making futures trading accessible to traders of all levels.
How does the Leader-Follower program work? The Leader-Follower program allows you to automatically mirror the trades of experienced professionals who trade with their own capital on the line. You select the leaders you want to follow, set your risk parameters, and trades are executed automatically in your account.
Which trading platforms do you support? We support 25+ trading platforms including NinjaTrader, TradingView, Sierra Chart, Rithmic R|Trader, Bookmap, MultiCharts, and many more. Each platform offers unique features to match different trading styles and strategies.
What kind of customer support do you offer? We provide dedicated customer support via phone, email, and live chat. Our team consists of knowledgeable trading professionals who can assist with platform setup, account questions, and technical issues. We pride ourselves on fast response times and genuine care for our clients.
