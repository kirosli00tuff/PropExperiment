01
What is Tradovate and how does it work with Phidias?
Tradovate is a cloud-based futures trading platform owned by NinjaTrader Group.
It connects natively to TradingView with its own built-in data feed. When you select Tradovate as your platform on Phidias, you can chart on TradingView and execute trades directly on your funded account. No extra software, no workarounds.
02
How do I connect TradingView to my Tradovate account?
Open TradingView, go to the Trading Panel, select Tradovate, and sign in.
Your Phidias funded account will appear automatically. Tradovate integrates natively with TradingView. No third-party bridges or plugins needed. The whole process takes under 2 minutes.
03
Is Tradovate available on all account types?
Yes. Tradovate is available on Express to Live, Fundamental, Premium, and Challenge accounts.
All rules, drawdown types, and payout structures stay the same regardless of platform. Your choice of platform only affects how you access the markets, not your trading conditions.
04
How does Tradovate connect to TradingView?
Natively. Tradovate has built-in TradingView integration with its own data feed.
Select Tradovate as your broker in TradingView's Trading Panel, sign in, and your funded account appears. Real-time data, no delays, no third-party bridges.
05
Can I switch between Tradovate and Rithmic?
Each account is tied to the platform you select at purchase.
You cannot switch an existing account between platforms. However, you can run multiple accounts on different platforms at the same time. Many traders use Tradovate for TradingView execution and Rithmic for algo trading or scalping.
06
Does Tradovate cost extra?
No. Same price as Rithmic and DeepCharts. Zero additional platform fees.
Your account pricing depends only on the type and size you choose. Tradovate, Rithmic, and DeepCharts are all included at no extra cost.
07
What order types does Tradovate support?
Market, limit, stop, stop-limit, OCO, OSO, trailing stops, auto breakeven, and auto trail.
All of these work on both the Tradovate platform and through TradingView. Professional-grade order management with no limitations compared to Rithmic.
08
Can I use Tradovate on mobile?
Yes. Full-featured apps for iOS and Android, plus any web browser.
Your watchlists, layouts, and workspaces sync across all devices automatically. Set up your charts on desktop, manage positions from your phone.
09
How does the built-in trade copier work?
Place a trade on one account and it copies to all your other Tradovate accounts in real time.
If you run multiple Phidias accounts (up to 15), the built-in copier eliminates the need for third-party tools. One trade, all accounts, instant execution.
10
What is the difference between Tradovate and Rithmic?
Rithmic is for speed. Tradovate is for flexibility.
Rithmic provides ultra-low latency direct market access, ideal for scalpers and algo traders. Tradovate offers native TradingView integration, cloud-based access on any device, and a built-in trade copier. Both are professional-grade and fast enough for 95%+ of traders.
11
What is the profit split when I trade on Tradovate?
Up to 100% profit split on Premium accounts. Same on Tradovate as on any other platform.
The profit split is tied to your account type, not the platform you choose. Express to Live and Fundamental accounts use a fixed 80/20 split (you keep 80%). Premium accounts use a progressive split that climbs from 75% to 100% as you stack payouts: 75% on payout 1, then 80%, 85%, 90%, and 
100% from payout 5 onwards
. Most prop firms cap traders at 80% or 90%.
