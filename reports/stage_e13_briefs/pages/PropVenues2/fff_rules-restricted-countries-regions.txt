These restrictions are in place due to various compliance, legal, and regulatory reasons. If you are located in one of these countries / regions, unfortunately, you will not be able to open or participate in any accounts with us.
If your country is not listed in our restricted countries / regions, you are eligible to participate in our trading platform. However, please note that for certain countries, while you may be able to access and trade on a simulated funded account, you may not be able to transition to a live funded account due to regulatory and compliance limitations.
These measures ensure we adhere to legal requirements and provide a compliant and secure trading environment for all users.
