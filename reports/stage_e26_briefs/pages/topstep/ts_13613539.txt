Position Limit Adjustments
Markets get wild sometimes. Expanding price limits. Velocity Logic halts. Historic ranges in minutes. When that happens, even experienced Traders can get caught on the wrong side fast — and fast losses can end a trading career before it really starts.
During extreme volatility, we may temporarily tighten position limits on affected products. We're talking about conditions like:
Expanding price limits
Velocity Logic halts
Historic price ranges
Rapid, sustained price movement
This isn't punishment. It's protection — guardrails to keep you in the game when the market is doing something it's never done before. Our job is to help you trade another day.
How Position Limits Are Adjusted
When a product is deemed high-risk due to volatility, we may implement one or both of the following adjustments:
Micro Contract Limits
Mini Contract Restrictions
Trading on mini-sized contracts or larger may be temporarily halted for affected products

When Do These Restrictions Apply?
Position limit adjustments are:
Temporary: Restrictions remain in place only while volatility levels are elevated
Product-specific: Only products experiencing extreme volatility are affected
Actively monitored: Our Risk Team continuously evaluates market conditions to determine when normal limits can be reinstated
What You Can Still Do
Continue trading with adjusted position sizes in affected products
Trade other products with standard position limits
Focus on risk management and strategic opportunities
Use this time to refine your trading plan and review your strategy
Understanding Market Volatility
Historic volatility means historic losses can happen faster than normal. When markets move unpredictably:
Slippage increases
Risk management becomes more challenging
Even well-planned trades can move against you quickly
These temporary adjustments give you the opportunity to participate in volatile markets while managing your risk appropriately.
How You'll Be Notified
When position limits are adjusted, you'll receive notification through:
Email communication
Dashboard banners
Social media updates (@Topstep on X)
We'll also notify you when restrictions are lifted and normal position limits are reinstated.
Tier 1 News Events Restrictions
Ahead of Tier 1 News Events (for example, CPI, FOMC, NFP, PPI), Topstep temporarily restricts new opening positions on equity index products and metals in SIM to protect Traders from extreme volatility.
Minis (ES, RTY, YM, NQ, NKD, GC, SI, HG, PL): No new opening transactions permitted during the 10-minute window surrounding the release (5 minutes before, 5 minutes after).
Micros (MES, M2K, MYM, MNQ, MGC): Opening transactions limited to 1, 3, 6, 9, and 15 contracts for the $25K, $50K, $100K, $150K, and $250K account sizes, respectively, during the window (see table below)
MHG and SIL: Opening transactions limited to 1, 2, 5, 7, 12 contracts for the $25K, $50K, $100K, $150K, and $250K account sizes, respectively, during the window (see table below)
Other products (energies, rates, FX, etc.) are unaffected.
Existing Positions: Traders holding existing positions in equity index products and metals prior to 5 minutes before the news event may keep them open and manage them through the Tier 1 News Events window. TopstepX™ automatically enforces these limits, so no manual adjustment is required for existing trades.
Pending orders: Pending orders are not open positions. They will not fill during the 10-minute window, and new pending orders intended to execute during this timeframe cannot be placed. TP/SL brackets that are reducing/closing positions will be accepted during the window.
How you'll be alerted: we'll email you and you'll see a banner at the top of the platform, so you're always prepared.
