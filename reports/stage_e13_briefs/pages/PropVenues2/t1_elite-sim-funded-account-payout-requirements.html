🏆 Elite Sim Funded Account: Payout Requirements

Congratulations on your Elite Sim Funded Account! This account is designed to reward consistent, profitable traders. To be eligible to request a payout, you must meet three key requirements.

Congratulations on your Elite Sim Funded Account! This account is designed to reward consistent, profitable traders. To be eligible to request a payout, you must meet three key requirements: a profit target, the 25% consistency rule, and the maximum payout amount per request.

In short: reach the profit target (6% for your first payout, 5% for the second, 4% from the third onwards), keep your best day under 25% of total profit, and request no more than the maximum per withdrawal for your account size.

1. The Profit Target

You must reach a specific profit target to be eligible for each payout. This target is dynamic, meaning it changes for your first three payouts to encourage consistent growth.

Payout

Second payout

5% on top of your new balance (after the first payout is deducted)

Third payout (and all future payouts)

4% on top of your new balance (after the previous payout is deducted)

2. The 25% Consistency Rule

This is a key performance metric. It ensures that your profits are earned steadily, rather than from a single "lucky" day. The rule: your single best trading day's profit cannot account for more than 25% of your total accumulated profit at the time of your payout request. The formula: (Best Day's Profit / Total Profit) x 100 = Your Consistency Score.

"I failed... how do I fix my score?" If your best day is too high, you must "fix" your score by accumulating more profits. You need to make that best day equal 25% of a new, larger total profit. Using Example 2:

  • Your best day is $1,000.

  • To find the profit target you need: $1,000 / 0.25 = $4,000

  • You must grow your total profit to $4,000 (without having another day better than $1,000) to meet the 25% rule.
