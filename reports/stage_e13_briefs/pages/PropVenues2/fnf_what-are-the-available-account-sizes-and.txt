TL;DR: 
FundedNext Futures offers multiple account sizes across different challenge models, including 
$25K, $50K, $100K, and $150K
 accounts. Pricing varies depending on the selected challenge type — 
Rapid Pro and Daily, Legacy, 
or 
Flex
—with each model offering different trading conditions, rules, and features. The 
Flex Account
 also includes a 
95% Reward Share 
for traders seeking higher profit potential.
1. Rapid Pro and Daily Challenge
The 
FundedNext Futures Rapid Pro & Daily Challenge
 is designed for traders who want speed and flexibility in a single challenge, with a fast one-day pass, an End-of-Day trailing drawdown, and two selectable paths — Rapid Pro and Rapid Daily. The Rapid Challenge is available in multiple account sizes, allowing traders to choose the path and model that best suit their trading style and risk management preferences.

How does Flex offer work?
Here's how Flex pricing works. The first five purchases made with the coupon get the best pricing: $69.99 at 50K, $129.99 at 100K, and $249.99 at 150K. From the sixth purchase onward, the same coupon applies a different discount, bringing the price to $79.99, $149.99, and $289.99.
4. Legacy Challenge
The 
FundedNext Futures Legacy Challenge
 is designed for traders who prefer a more structured trading environment with disciplined risk management. It includes a 
Consistency Rule during the Challenge Phase
, encouraging traders to maintain stable and consistent trading performance throughout their evaluation journey.
