Prime Services Indices

Our Capital Consulting team manages a family of non-investable indices designed to represent a number of key hedge fund strategy styles.

Please contact our Capital Consulting team for more information about any of the Societe Generale Prime Services Indices or to inquire about requirements for inclusion of a strategy into one of the indices.

Daily indices are calculated T+1 and published by 2pm US Eastern time.  Monthly indices are initially calculated mid-month.

SG Prime Services index data is provided on this website for information purposes only, initial calculations are estimates and may be subsequently revised.

To license this data commercially, reference the index returns, or access full daily data, please contact the Capital Consulting team or request access via the SG Markets Analytics & Data platform

SG Multi Alternative Risk Premia Index
Apr 23, 2015-0.81%1.87%View

The SG Multi Alternative Risk Premia Index represents risk premia managers who employ investment programs diversified across multiple asset classes while utilizing multiple risk premia factors. These managers trade multiple asset classes such as equities, fixed income, currencies, and in many cases commodities, and aim to capture a diversity of discrete risk premia, including most prevalently value, carry, and momentum. These multi-asset, multi-risk premia strategies are typically systematic. Single asset class and risk premia programs are excluded. The SG Multi Alternative Risk Premia Index is an equally weighed, non-investable index of funds.

Bloomberg: NEIXMARP Index

The SG Macro Trading Index is a broad based performance measure for constituents that trade Global Macro strategies. These managers may typically employ top-down fundamental research to forecast the effect of global macroeconomic and political events on the valuation of financial instruments and are frequently focused on a diversified basket of instruments.

In order to be eligible for inclusion as a constituent program, individual programs must predominately trade a relevant Global Macro strategy, provide monthly performance data, and have AUM greater than USD 30 million.

SG Commodity Trading Index (Trading)
Mar 2015-0.49%-0.32View 

The SG Commodity Trading Index (Trading) is a broad based performance measure for constituents covering Commodity trading orientated strategies, typically involving the trading of physical commodity products and/or of commodity derivative instruments. Commodity funds utilize a variety of investment strategies to profit from price moves in commodity markets, typically either directional or relative value strategies.

In order to be eligible for inclusion as a constituent program, individual programs must predominately trade a relevant Commodity strategy, provide monthly performance data, and have AUM greater than USD 30 million.
