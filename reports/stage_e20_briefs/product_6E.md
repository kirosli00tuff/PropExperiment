**Reading of decay and costs.**

- Market intraday momentum does not hold for Euro FX. Baltussen et al.'s own Euro FX row is a null (beta_ROD -0.04, t -0.17, out-of-sample R2 -0.07%). The currency class is insignificant in 2000-2020 (0.33, t 1.45), and the class portfolio earned 0.85%/yr, about 0.34 bp (0.75 ticks) a day, a third of the 6E wall. The Aalto thesis (2008-2024, abstract only, shared) says currency-futures momentum and reversal strategies are mostly not feasible after costs.
- The fix-driven intraday seasonality (Krohn et al.) is the strongest published FX evidence since 2015 (JF 2024, t up to 11.7). It decays across subsamples: the dollar portfolio's post-London-fix move fell from 2.80 bp (1999-2003) to 0.86 bp (2014-2019, t 1.62). The CME-futures version is 0.64 bp (2006-2019). The authors find liquidity-demanding trades lose after costs. The part inside the 6E day session (post-London fix, 10:00-16:00 CT) is smaller than the 6E wall on the recent subsample. The pre-ECB-fix leg (01:00-07:15 CT) is larger but falls before t1.
- The fix events changed during the windows: the WMR fix window widened from 1 to 5 minutes on 15 February 2015 (BIS, Debelle 2015: 'WM has subsequently announced that it will widen the fix window to five minutes commencing on 15 February'), and the ECB moved reference-rate publication to 16:00 CET in July 2016, which shifted the EUR turning point from 14:15 to 14:00 CET (Krohn et al. Table VI). K3-ecbfix's T_E = 14:15 Berlin is the pre-2016 turning point. Evans (2018) and Melvin-Prins (2013/2015) use pre-2015 samples, before the window change.
- Release windows: no post-2015 peer-reviewed, sized evidence was found for a EUR/USD drift of 60 minutes or more after NFP (07:30 CT, before t1) or ECB press conferences. A 2026 study finds no drift after ECB inter-meeting communication (E8). The FOMC-day currency pattern (E7) uses 12-hour windows over 1999-2015 and is partly reversed within 24 hours. FOMC and NFP are already in 6E's g13-g15 list.
- V34's drops (overnight drift, closing auction, unsized pre-announcement drift, noise-area momentum) are not re-proposed.
- Screened out: Li, Sakkas, Urquhart (JFM 2022) is equity-only (saved, `pages/6E/li_sakkas_urquhart_reading95566.pdf`); RTY's domain. Elaut, Frommel, Lampaert (JFM 2018) is RUB/USD, not fetched.

## 3. Proposed features

Two are proposed. Neither clears 1.5x on the published evidence as an unconditional expectation; the lead decides.

### `p6e_mehedge_nq`

- Source: Melvin and Prins (2015, JFM 22:50-72), 2013 ECB-workshop draft, pp.13-14 (E6).
- Quote: 'an equity market appreciation over the month predicts a statistically significant depreciation in the currency in the hour leading up to the end of month fix. The implied magnitude is that a 10% equity appreciation leads to 14 basis points of currency depreciation.' / 'At each period, we subtract the cross-section mean return of currencies against the USD.'
- Formula: On trade date d = ME(m), the last FX trade date of calendar month m (FX group calendar; hist2010/fx.json before 2019-05, data/calendars/fx.py after), at t1 = 07:50 CT: value = ln( C_NQ(e_prev) / C_NQ(e_base) ), where C_NQ(x) is the close of NQ's last 1-min bar ending at or before 15:00 CT (equity group C) on equity trade date x, e_prev = the last equity trade date strictly before d's calendar date, e_base = the last equity trade date of month m-1. On every other date or decision time: 0 and flag 0. Sign: positive value predicts 6E up (USD depreciation) in the 09:00-10:00 CT pre-fix hour, because in the paper's demeaned panel EUR/USD's predicted return is +0.0142 x (r_US_eq - r_EU_eq) and only the US leg is among the 17 roots. Differences from the paper: NQ stands in for the US total-market index; the euro-area equity leg is missing (no free EURO STOXX 50 history, and it is not one of the 17 roots); the base is the previous month's last close, as in K3-mehedge, while the paper says 'from the first to the second last day of the month'.
- Decision times: t1; horizons: h120.
- Legs: NQ, 6E (traded). Calendars: FX group calendar (month ends): hist2010/fx.json 2010-06-01..2019-05-31, data/calendars/fx.py 2019-05-01..2026-06-19; equity group calendar for NQ closes: hist2010/equity.json 2010-06-01..2019-05-31, data/calendars/equity.py. Available both windows: True.
- Expected gross per trade: 2.63 ticks against the D8 wall of 2.305 ticks ($14.41); ratio 1.14; clears 1.5x: False.
- Derivation: Expected figure and ratio are per 1% of relative month-to-date equity return (conditional slope, not an unconditional mean). 1.42 bp per 1% of relative (US minus euro-area) month-to-date equity return over the full 60-min pre-fix hour; the t1/h120 hold (07:50-09:50 CT) overlaps 50 of those 60 min in standard weeks (pro rata 1.18 bp) and none of them in C9 mismatch weeks (T_L 11:00 CT). 1.18 bp / 0.4505 bp per tick = 2.63 ticks per 1% of relative return. 1.5 x D8 = 3.458 ticks (1.56 bp) needs a relative return of at least 1.32%. NQ alone measures the relative return only partly (the euro-area leg is missing), so the realized slope on the NQ-only value is smaller than 0.0142 by an unknown factor. The unconditional mean per trade is not derivable from the paper. About 12 events a year (about 106 in the backward window, 58 in the search window).

### `p6e_post_ldnfix`

- Source: Krohn, Mueller and Whelan (2024, JF 79(1) 541-578), Table I and p.10 (E2).
- Quote: 'After the London fix, the pattern is again quite striking: with the exception of the yen, all currencies appreciate strongly (i.e., between 1.5 bps for the Canadian dollar and 2.7 bps for the euro) during the period between the London fix and the close of markets in the U.S.'
- Formula: On every 6E row: T_L(d) = 16:00 Europe/London on calendar date d converted to CT with zoneinfo (10:00 CT, 11:00 CT in C9 mismatch weeks; same rule as k3/_clocks.py, extended to 2010-2019). Value at decision time t = minutes elapsed since T_L(d) if t >= T_L(d), applicability flag 1; else 0 and flag 0. Sign: positive applicability predicts 6E up to F (USD depreciates from the London fix to 17:00 ET).
- Decision times: t2, t3, t4; horizons: F, h60, h120.
- Legs: 6E (traded); no bars read. Calendars: T_L by rule (zoneinfo Europe/London and America/Chicago); FX group calendar both windows. Available both windows: True.
- Expected gross per trade: 1.53 ticks against the D8 wall of 2.305 ticks ($14.41); ratio 0.66; clears 1.5x: False.
- Derivation: Expected figure uses the latest published subsample (dollar portfolio 2014-2019, the closest to the search window). Full sample 1999-2019: EUR post-L 2.73 bp over 10:00-16:00 CT; t2 to F (10:20-15:08 CT, 4.8 of 6 h, pro rata) 2.18 bp = 4.85 ticks, ratio 2.10 to D8. Latest published subsample (dollar portfolio 2014-2019) 0.86 bp, pro rata 0.69 bp = 1.53 ticks, ratio 0.66. CME futures dollar portfolio 2006-2019 0.64 bp, pro rata 0.51 bp = 1.14 ticks, ratio 0.49. Fails 1.5x on the recent and on the futures-based evidence; the authors report liquidity-demander returns negative after costs. Overlap: away from mismatch weeks the value is a function of the decision time, which G11 together with the product identifier already carries.

Not proposed: an ECB-fix feature (the published effect lasts 15 minutes, below the 60-minute minimum hold, and its timing moved in 2016); a post-NFP or post-ECB-press-conference drift (no sized post-2015 primary evidence); the pre-ECB-fix leg (01:00-07:15 CT, unreachable at t1-t4).
