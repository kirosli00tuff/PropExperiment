Explore CSI's Unfair Advantage software for 21 days. You have access to any 5 US Futures within the CME and any 10 US Stocks within the Nasdaq with 18 months of history included for 21 day access.  Silver Packages
For monthly subscriptions: Access to 5 years of historical data is provided for each selected category, except the $20 Trial which has 18 months. For yearly subscriptions: Access to 10 years of historical data is provided for each selected category. Each month of daily updates extends the accessible history by one additional month beyond your history's initial start date. Each of our offerings for Personal and Private Use customers includes a very liberal cap on the maximum number of markets that may be processed through Unfair Advantage's portfolios on any given day. These limits are well beyond the expected use of this product by individual investors and analysts. For auditing purposes, a "market" is defined as a specific commodity or other similar tradable offered by an individual exchange, such as LME tin or CBT gold. These limits do not prevent you from exploring any of the markets involved in your update categories, as long as the cumulative daily total doesn't exceed the stated caps. When early access is used (before 6:30 p.m.), charting is limited to 250 series. After 6:30 p.m., charting is unlimited. *Silver Stock packages are limited to 7,500 stocks/mutual funds/indices when bought separately or combined.
