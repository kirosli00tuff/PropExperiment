XFA Activation — Trading Combine®
This article covers XFA activation for traders with a passed 50K, 100K, or 150K
Trading Combine®
(account prefix: 50KTC, 100KTC, or 150KTC). Activation is done directly from your Trade Report. Depending on the subscription path you chose, there may or may not be an activation fee.
What to Expect After Passing
You'll receive a confirmation email from Topstep
Your Trade Report will show a "Start Express Funded Account!" button on the right side — click it to begin
It may take up to 30 minutes for your Trading Combine status to reflect as passed in your dashboard
You'll see your Funded Trader Certificate in the
Achievements
tab of your Topstep Dashboard!
Starting Balance
Your Express Funded Account starts with a
$0 balance
— this is expected and correct. The account size you see (e.g. $50,000) is your
buying power
, not a cash balance. Your Trading Combine balance does not carry over to your XFA. Your balance grows from $0 as you earn profits from trading.
☝️ If you already have 5 active XFAs or a Live Funded Account®, the button will be greyed out. You'll need to close an account before activating a new one.

Steps to Activate
From the
Trade Report
of your passed Trading Combine®, click
"Activate Express Funded Account."
If you have multiple passed Trading Combines, confirm the correct account before clicking.
​
Select your XFA type
— Standard or Consistency. Learn more:
XFA Parameters
.
​
Account Info:
Review the Trading Combine name you're activating and confirm it's correct.
​
Review Agreement:
Read the XFA Agreement document and click
Continue
.
​
Agreement Signed:
Check both boxes to confirm you've read and agreed to the terms. Click
"Agree and Continue."
​
Activation Fee:
No Activation Fee path:
Click Continue — your account is created!
Standard path:
Check all 3 fee agreement boxes, enter your payment info (credit or debit card), and click
"Complete Payment to Activate."
​
Account Created:
You'll see a "Tips from our Coaches" page. The left side shows a confirmation of each completed step. Click
"Finish"
to return to your dashboard and your new XFA.
