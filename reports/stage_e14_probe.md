## Method

- Energy: CME's Holiday Calendar page (Wayback captures 2012-06-16 and 2012-11-05) lists every 2012 CME Globex holiday window with a PDF schedule (New Year's 2012, MLK, Presidents, Good Friday, Memorial, Independence, Labor, Columbus, Veterans, Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's 2013). All 12 PDFs were read from Wayback (saved under pages/cme_holiday/, pdftotext alongside); row read: "NYMEX, COMEX® and DME Products on CME Globex" (Good Friday: "NYMEX & COMEX® and DME Products on CME Globex"). CME's own 2012-holiday-calendars.zip (saved by the CalendarBuilder workers) holds the same 2012 set (14 files) and was used to cross-check Christmas 2012 and New Year 2013, whose .doc versions postdate the PDFs read (same NYMEX rows).
- Unscheduled events: CME's list of its 2012 CFTC rule filings has exactly two Rule 257 emergency-action filings, both Hurricane Sandy (12-363 for Oct 29, 12-370 for Oct 30); both were read on cftc.gov with CME Clearing advisories 12-463 and 12-464. NYMEX closed its floor on Oct 29-30; CME Globex energy stayed available in regular hours, so Oct 29 and 30 are normal energy trade dates. Limit: CME's weekly Globex notices were not read one by one for technical outages (one web search for a 2012 NYMEX Globex outage, cmegroup.com and cftc.gov only, found none).
- Session: CME Globex Sunday-Friday 17:00-16:15 CT with a 45-minute break (CL and NG contract specifications, June 2012 captures; every 2012 holiday schedule states the NYMEX regular close 1615 CT and regular open 1700 CT). Note this differs from energy.py's 2019+ session (17:00-16:00 CT).
- Holiday halts in 2012 are 12:15 CT ("1215 CT / 1315 ET – Trading halt"), not 12:00 as in 2019-2021; day-after-Thanksgiving and Christmas Eve close 12:45 CT. The holiday-halt session is kept as its own short trade date (energy.py convention) although CME books it to the next trade date.
- NGS: EIA's WNGSR holiday release schedule (ir.eia.gov/ngs/schedule.html, Wayback 2012-01-01 and 2013-01-09; both show the same 2012 rows) gives the standing rule and the 2012 exceptions. Every distinct 2011-12..2013-01 Wayback capture of ir.eia.gov/ngs/ngs.html (CDX, collapsed by digest) was read for "Released: ... for the Week Ending ..." and "Next Release:". For the 26 weeks with no report capture, EIA's Natural Gas Weekly Update of the scheduled day (eia.gov archive, fetched directly; EIA content is public domain) was read for its release date and its storage sentence ("... as of Friday, <date>, according to EIA's WNGSR"). EIA's notice of WNGSR changes (Nov 15, 2012) lists test publications of old data on 2012-11-27, 11-30, 12-04 and 12-07 at 11:00 a.m. on ir.eia.gov/ngs_test/ngs.html: these are not releases and are not rows.
