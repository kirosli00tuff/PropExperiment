## Implementation choices (none can change a trade)

1. pmfix and fomc share one rule object, `_event_common.SignedMoveEvent` (plan per date: earlier signal bar, entry
   decision bar = later signal bar, exit decision bar); pmfix.py and fomc.py hold only their constants, schedule and
   table. preauc has its own `on_minute`, the shape of K4's audited ngpre.
2. `_event_common.py` copies K4's helpers (clock_minute, ct_open_ns, price_ticks, exit_items, check_exposure) into k5;
   nothing is imported from k2 or k4.
3. Each member takes its table as a dataclass field defaulting to the frozen table (am_auctions, pm_auctions,
   statement_dates), so a test can inject a moved or dropped row; the factories pass nothing.
4. The early-halt and pending checks come after `_entry_done = True` on the exact entry bar (as K4): one chance per
   trade date.
5. The auction tables carry 3 rows a line (ruff's 100-character limit), so _releases.py is 1,295 lines.
6. NO_AUCTION_DAYS' reason text is the check's reason plus its LBMA notice date.
7. Two test files (tests/test_e4_k5_members_b.py and ..._b_signal.py); the second imports the first's synthetic kit.
   D9.7 is shown with a test-only `ForcedLimitRules` subclass (MGC is DCB-only, not in HARD_LIMIT_PRODUCTS, so the
   real D9.7 exit cannot fire on it), as in K4's tests.
8. Mutation check (scratch, not kept): 15 single-line mutations of the member code (offsets, side, instrument guard,
   s = 0, sign, early halt, per-date reset, pending-exit check, exit timing, one-entry flag) each made at least one
   test fail; every file was restored and its hash asserted.
