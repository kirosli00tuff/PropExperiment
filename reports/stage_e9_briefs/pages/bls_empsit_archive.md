Employment Situation Archived News Releases
===========================================

[Archived News Releases](/bls/news-release/home.htm) | The Employment Situation
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Employment Situation news release presents data from the [Current Employment Statistics](https://www.bls.gov/ces/) and [Current Population Survey](https://www.bls.gov/cps/) programs.

#### Caution

Data in archived news releases may have been revised in subsequent releases. The latest data, including any revisions, may be obtained from the databases accessible on the program homepages:

* [Current Employment Statistics: databases](https://www.bls.gov/ces/#data)
* [Current Population Survey: databases](https://www.bls.gov/cps/#data)
