Can I Trade News?
Our news trading policy: news trading is allowed on all accounts, with specific rules for Elite Daily and Elite ACCESS funded accounts.

At Top One Futures, we understand the importance of 
news events
 in influencing market movements. As part of our commitment to providing a flexible and trader-friendly environment, we 
allow news trading
 on your accounts. This article explains the policy and the exceptions.
Can I trade news on your accounts?
Yes
, you are allowed to trade news on your accounts at Top One Futures. We recognize that news events can create significant market opportunities, and we encourage traders to take advantage of these movements.
Are there any restrictions on news trading?
While we allow news trading, we expect traders to manage their risk carefully. Volatile market conditions during major news events can lead to significant price swings, so we recommend implementing proper risk management strategies such as stop-loss orders, position sizing, and trade timing.

What should I keep in mind when trading news?
Risk management:
 news events can lead to rapid and unpredictable price movements. Always use appropriate risk management techniques.
Volatility:
 expect higher-than-usual volatility during major news releases. Be prepared for sudden market swings.
Market hours:
 make sure you are aware of the timing of important news events, and plan your trades accordingly.
By allowing news trading, Top One Futures provides traders with the tools and flexibility to trade effectively in a dynamic market environment. Always remember to trade responsibly and manage your risk.
