## Judgment calls (all mine, for the lead to accept or overrule)

1. Holiday sessions CME books to the next trade date. Before 2019 CME assigned the Monday-holiday session (and the Thanksgiving Thursday session) to the NEXT trade date ("Day/Session orders entered on Sunday are for trade date Tuesday"). Each such holiday is kept as its own short trade date ending at halt_ct, the data.cme_calendar convention for 2019+. Equity/rates/FX trade-date counts therefore include those holidays.
2. Equity regime S1 (to 2012-11-16): CME's schedules say the 15:30 CT open starts the NEXT trade date, so trade date D = 15:30-16:30 CT on D-1 plus 17:00 D-1 to 15:15 D. SessionSpec offsets cannot express that this prior-day segment is absent on Monday trade dates and after closures or holiday halts; recorded in the session note, not as entries.
3. Hurricane Sandy. Equity 2012-10-29 halt 08:15 CT from the CME/CBOT emergency self-certification on cftc.gov (a CME document, graded cme); 2012-10-30 halt 08:15 CT from CME Clearing Advisory 12-464; 2012-10-31 normal per the CME press release of 2012-10-30. Rates 2012-10-29 halt 11:00 CT, 10-30 normal. FX and grains normal on both days on the strength of Chadv12-464 "All other CME Group futures and options on futures markets will remain open." (dated 10-29). Under S1, equity trade dates 10-30 and 10-31 lacked the 15:30-16:30 prior-day segment (noted, no extra entry).
4. 2018-12-05 national day of mourning: equity halt 08:30 CT, rates closed, FX and grains normal (CME press release 2018-12-02).
5. Columbus and Veterans Days. 2010-2012: CME schedules show regular Globex sessions on the day for all four groups; rates and FX had a 15:15 CT early close on the Friday before Columbus Day (entries). 2013-2017: CME's schedule for each says "Products listed on Globex are unaffected and will run on a normal schedule"; I took that to cover the preceding Friday too (no rates/FX Friday early close entered for 2013-10-11 etc.), although 2010-2012 had one. 2018: CME settlement notices "All products will settle at their normal times".
6. Grain closures inferred from schedule gaps.
