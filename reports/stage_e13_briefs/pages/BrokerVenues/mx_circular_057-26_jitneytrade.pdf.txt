CIRCULAR 057-26

                         ADMISSION OF AN APPROVED PARTICIPANT
                                   JITNEYTRADE INC.




On May 1, 2026, the application of Jitneytrade Inc. to join Montréal Exchange Inc. (the
“Exchange”) as an Approved Participant was approved by the Self-Regulatory Oversight
Committee of the Exchange. The head office of Jitneytrade Inc. is located at 360, rue Saint-
Jacques, Suite G102, Montréal, QC H2Y 1P5 (Telephone: 514-985-8080).

Jitneytrade Inc. is registered as an Investment Dealer with the Canadian Investment Regulatory
Organization (CIRO) and the Canadian Security Administrators in most provinces and territories.
The firm is also registered as a Derivatives Dealer with the Autorité des marchés financiers
(AMF) and as a Futures Commission Merchant with the Ontario Securities Commission (OSC)
and the Manitoba Securities Commission (MSC).
