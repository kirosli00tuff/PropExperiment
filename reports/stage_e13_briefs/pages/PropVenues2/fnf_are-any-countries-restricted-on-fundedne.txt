TL;DR
: FundedNext Futures restricts access for residents of certain countries due to regulatory, compliance, and operational requirements. These restrictions apply to purchasing accounts and are subject to change. For traders reaching live eligibility who reside in certain countries, our Risk Desk will contact you to review your options.

Why are these restrictions in place?
The decision to restrict access is based on regulatory requirements, compliance considerations, and operational feasibility. Our primary goal is to ensure a smooth and legally compliant trading experience for our global community.
FundedNext Futures continuously reviews these restrictions and may update them based on evolving regulations.
If your country is currently restricted or if regulations change in the future, FundedNext may adjust its access policies. Stay informed by regularly checking our official announcements or contacting our support team for the latest updates.
