Can I open an AMP account if I live outside the United States?

AMP
  • July 15, 2025 22:35
  • Updated
Follow

Yes, you can open an AMP account even if you live outside the United States, but there are a few important points to know:

Eligibility for International Clients

  1. Most countries allowed
    AMP welcomes clients from many countries around the world and enables global account opening via an online application on our website.
  2. Some countries restricted
    AMP is unable to open accounts for residents or entities in certain high-risk countries. These include places like Afghanistan, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Russia, parts of Africa, and others. You can find the full list in their “Restricted Countries List” here:  Restricted Countries List

How It Works

  • Access
    To start, go to AMP’s website and complete the online application—available globally 24/7 - Apply Now
  • Approval process
    AMP’s new accounts team will verify your country of residence based on your documents and decide if you’re eligible.

Summary


If your country isn’t on AMP’s restricted list, you’re clear to apply.  AMP supports global account opening. Please check the restricted list first, and proceed accordingly.
