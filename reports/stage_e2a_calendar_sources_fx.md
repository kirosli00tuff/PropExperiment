## Method

CME's own documents, read through Wayback Machine copies (cmegroup.com refuses this machine), plus CME's Client Systems Wiki read directly. Text renderings for the check:
- xls: LibreOffice (soffice --headless --convert-to csv, field separator '|') export of every sheet, trailing empty cells dropped, sheets in file-name order; for 2019-2021 the .xls is a member of CME's yearly holiday-calendars.zip (status_member).
- pdf: poppler pdftotext -layout.
- json: the gunzipped body of CME's trading-hours-by-product response.
- html: script and style removed, tags stripped, HTML entities unescaped.
- png: no text layer; the one image source is transcribed and marked verbatim_check false.
- Normalization: zero-width characters (U+200B, U+200C, U+200D, U+FEFF) removed, no-break spaces made spaces, every run of whitespace (spaces, tabs, newlines) collapsed to one space, ends stripped; a quote's fragments are split on ' ... ' and must occur in the normalized text in order.
