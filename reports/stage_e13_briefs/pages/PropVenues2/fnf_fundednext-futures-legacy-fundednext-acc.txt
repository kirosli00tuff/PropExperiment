 On the 
Legacy FundedNext Account

A 
Performance Reward
 is your share of the profits you earn for your skill and effort while trading a 
FundedNext Account

Performance Reward

Is there a Consistency Rule on the Legacy FundedNext Account?
No.
 Unlike the Challenge Phase, there is 
no Consistency Rule
 on the Legacy FundedNext Account. You have full flexibility in how much you earn on any given day.

Is a buffer required on the Legacy FundedNext Account?
No.
 The Legacy FundedNext Account does 
not
 use a buffer rule. Withdrawal eligibility is based on 
Benchmark Days
 instead (see below). The buffer rule applies only to the Rapid Daily model.

How much can I withdraw, and how often, from the Legacy FundedNext Account?
Before completing 30 Benchmark Days, you can withdraw up to 
50% of your accumulated profit
 per cycle, capped by size (
$3,000 / $6,000 / $6,000
), with a 
$250
 minimum and at least 
$500 profit
 in the current cycle.
After 30 Benchmark Days
, both the 50% limit and the cap are removed — you can withdraw up to 
100% of your simulated profit
.
Important:
 Withdrawing 
all
 of your simulated profit in a single request causes a 
hard breach
.

What is the Legacy FundedNext Account withdrawal journey?
Trade and accumulate at least 
5 Benchmark Days
 and 
$500 profit
 in the cycle.
Request a withdrawal (minimum 
$250
, up to 50% of profit capped by size), receiving an 
80% Reward Share
.
Your 
first withdrawal
 resets the MLL to your initial balance.
Continue building Benchmark Days. Once you reach 
30 Benchmark Days
, the 50% limit and cap are removed, unlocking 
100% withdrawals
 for all future cycles.
Unlike the other models, the Legacy FundedNext Account is 
not concluded after 5 Performance Rewards
 — the 30-Benchmark-Day milestone unlocks full withdrawals and you can continue trading.

What is the Micro-Scalping policy for the Legacy FundedNext Account?
Micro-scalping
 is opening and closing trades within 
10 seconds
 to capture small, rapid price movements. FundedNext Futures monitors a 
Micro-Scalping threshold
 across all account types.
On your 
FundedNext Account
:
The Micro-Scalping percentage is the share of your 
total recorded profit
 from trades closed within 10 seconds, evaluated 
per cycle
.
Only profitable trades count
 — you can close losing trades any time without affecting this rule.
At 
30%
, you receive a 
compliance warning
 (no deduction).
At 
40% or above
, the Micro-Scalping portion of your profit for that cycle is 
deducted in full
. Your account is 
never terminated
 for micro-scalping, and future micro-scalping profit is subject to the same ongoing deduction.
If that deduction 
equals or exceeds your net profit
 for the cycle, 
no Performance Reward is issued
 and withdrawal is disabled for that cycle. A full breakdown appears in your dashboard.
🔗 
See: 
What is the Micro-Scalping policy in FundedNext Futures?
How do I request my Performance Reward from the Legacy FundedNext Account?
Once you've met all the eligibility criteria, you can 
request your Performance Reward at the end of the trading day
 by transferring your profits to your wallet.
Please note: 
placing a trade on the same day removes the transfer-to-wallet option for that day.
 So if you intend to request your Performance Reward, do it on a day when you haven't opened a new trade — otherwise you'll need to wait until the next eligible day to transfer.
