FAQ FAQ
Frequently asked questions
Quick answers to the most common questions about trading with GFF Brokers.
What is the Minimum Deposit to Open an Account? There is no minimum to open an account; you only need to deposit enough funds to cover the margins for the products and trades you plan to place.
Can I open multiple accounts? Yes, you can open multiple accounts to trade different strategies.
What is your Commission Rate? Because we offer a wide selection of platforms, support accounts with a variety of FCMs, offer multiple account types and various services, our brokers quote commissions individually. Contact us for a personalized quote.
What are your Margins? The Overnight/Full and Day Trading Margins are listed on our Margins page. Day Trading Margins vary depending on the FCM clearing firm you choose.
How can I fund my account? Funding instructions vary depending on the FCM/FDM. Most accounts can be funded via wire transfer, ACH, or check in a variety of currencies.
How can I withdraw funds? Withdrawal methods vary by FCM. Most support wire transfers and ACH. Visit our Withdrawal page or contact your broker for specific instructions.
What time do the markets close? Market hours are listed under each product on the Futures Products page. Most futures markets trade nearly 24 hours, Sunday through Friday.
Do you open international accounts? Yes. GFF Brokers works with traders around the world through our FCM partners. Country-specific options depend on the clearing firm's regulatory coverage - reach out and our team will match you with the right firm for your jurisdiction.
Can I trade with IRA funds? Yes, IRA accounts are available. Please notify your broker prior to account opening so we can guide you through the process.
What if my computer freezes during a trade? Our trade desk is available 24-hours a day. Call 866-249-4582 to place or manage orders by phone at any time.
Do you offer mobile platforms? Yes, many of our platforms are available as mobile apps, including: CQG Mobile, R|Trader, Tradingview and more.
What happens if I forget my username or password? Contact our tech support team at tech@gffbrokers.com or call 844-896-7432 for immediate assistance.
