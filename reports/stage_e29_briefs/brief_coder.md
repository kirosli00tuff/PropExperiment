## What to build (all under reports/stage_e29_briefs/work/basket/)
1. e29_basket.py: a new module. It imports E.28's modules by path, read-only (do not copy or edit them; record their
   sha256 in your log). It builds, per firm, basket, rho and edge setting, the combined daily mean M and SD S of the
   declaration's section 3 and feeds them to E.28's funnel exactly where E.28 feeds a single pair's daily mean and
   SD, on E.28's common random numbers (seed 20261021, tags "e28a3"). Because E.28 caches evaluations by d = mu - c
   for one pair, you will need an evaluation keyed by (M, S) instead; keep E.28's search (bisection on the integer
   grid index, f(lo) < 0 <= f(hi)) and its grid confirmation (A3_econ.md 2.4) unchanged in logic, applied to the
   grid of k (setting i) or mu_QM (setting ii).
2. The Topstep B3 sensitivity of declaration section 4 (MGC dropped on funded days at the 2-lot XFA tier). This needs
   a day-dependent (M, S) in Topstep's funded stage only; implement it in your module (a thin wrapper, as E.28's
   A3_funnel.py did for its extensions), never by editing prop_econ/ or E.28 files. Also report the share of B3
   funded days at the 2-lot tier (primary form).
3. The extended alone break-evens of declaration section 5 (MGC at Topstep, Tradeify, Bulenox; MNQ at MFFU, Bulenox;
   limit and market case) on a 0 to 12 tick grid with E.28's own single-pair model, same search and confirmation.
4. test_e29_basket.py (pytest, run directly on that file; it is not part of the repo suite). Required tests:
   (a) B0 reproduces E.28's QM results exactly at all five firms: mu*_lim, mu*_mkt, mu10K, cost per attempt, and
       every stored evaluation in pairs/<firm>__QM.json and gridcheck/<firm>__QM.json (bit for bit, or state the
       maximum absolute difference if any is not, and stop and report: that is a failure);
   (b) the combined (M, S) formula against a direct simulation of correlated member days (sum of draws) for one
       basket at rho 0.5, means and SDs within Monte Carlo error;
   (c) rho = 0 and one member at zero trades reduce to the other member alone;
   (d) the T split (B3 = 7, 7, 6) and the lot arithmetic (B3 2.1 lots);
   (e) the B3 sensitivity wrapper with the 2-lot tier never reached reduces exactly to the primary form.
5. e29_run.py: the driver. Cells: 5 firms x {B0} x 2 settings, plus 5 firms x {B1..B4} x rho {0, .25, .5, .75} x 2
   settings, limit case; then the market case for B0 and each firm's best basket (declaration section 6 rule,
   applied by the script from the limit results, logged). Per cell, every quantity of declaration section 6. Plus
   the Topstep B3 sensitivity (both settings, all rho). Resumable: one JSON per cell in
   work/basket/cells/, skipped when present and its fingerprint (code sha + inputs) matches.
6. Outputs: reports/stage_e29_briefs/E29_cells.json (one record per cell with every quantity, its grid check,
   provenance and runtime) and reports/stage_e29_briefs/E29_tables.md (generated by a script from the JSON: one table
   per firm, basket x rho x edge setting, with the B0 comparison columns of declaration section 7, then the market
   case table, then the extended break-evens, the sensitivity and the binding-limit log). No interpretation in the
   tables beyond the declaration's mechanical "beats B0" flag.
