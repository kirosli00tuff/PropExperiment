1. CME Non-Professional Data
----------------------------

This document is designed to help you determine if you qualify as a non-professional subscriber
under CME Group’s definition. Users who meet the criteria can access CME live data with a
Standard subscription plan.

You must satisfy each of CME's non-professional conditions, including using a supported platform
that can execute trades through your broker and maintaining a funded trading account.
These requirements are reflected in the eight questions below, and you’ll be asked to confirm
your responses when activating a license. Your declarations will be recorded in an attestation
form that you sign for regulatory compliance.

Read each description carefully, as it may include important clarifications or conditions that
affect eligibility.

To qualify, you must answer **YES** to all of the following:

**1. Are you an individual (natural person) or a small business entity?**
Your Databento account must be registered in the non-professional subscriber's name and not that
of a corporation, fund, or institution.

**2. Are you accessing the data for your own personal use?**
Real-time CME data may not be used on behalf or to the benefit of any other individual, employer,
trading group, or organization. External distribution, which includes use cases like publishing
real-time quotes on a website or application, is prohibited within 8 hours of receipt.
After 8 hours, the data is considered historical and no longer subject to these restrictions.
However, distributing any CME data via an API, whether real-time or historical, requires
an Information License Agreement (ILA) to be in place.

**3. Are you using the data solely to manage your own assets?**
You may not use real-time CME data to manage or advise on others’ assets in any capacity, such as
a fund manager, broker, or consultant.

**4. Are you accessing the data on two or fewer devices?**
CME imposes a two-device limit for non-professional subscribers.

**5. Do you have an active futures trading account with a broker?**
You must have a funded account with a broker for trading CME futures products.

**6. Are you using third-party software that can execute trades through your broker?**
Real-time CME data may only be used with software that can route orders and execute trades through
your broker. While you're not required to use a specific broker, the software platform or
application must be capable of receiving Databento data and integrating with your brokerage for
trade execution. Supported platforms include Architect Financial Technologies, Alpaca Securities,
Blue Trading Systems, Lightspeed Financial Services, Lime Trading, Multicharts, Nirvana Technology
Solutions, and Topstep.

**7. Do you confirm that you are not registered with any financial regulatory authority and not affiliated with any exchange?**
You must not be a member or leaseholder of any exchange or be registered or qualified as a
professional trader or investment adviser with any regulatory authority, e.g., SEC, CFTC, FINRA.

**8. Do you confirm that you have read and will abide by CME’s terms?**
You must attest that you’ve read and will abide by the current and future terms set forth by the
exchange in CME's [Schedule 5 to the ILA](https://to.dbn.to/cme-ila-schedule-5),
Paragraphs 6 and 8 and [Exhibit A to Schedule 4: Non-Professional Self-Certification Form](https://to.dbn.to/cme-nonpro-schedule-4).
