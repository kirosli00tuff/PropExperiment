Accounts will be assessed a separate market data subscription fee for each user that subscribes to data. Subscriptions are charged on a per username basis and subscriptions cannot be shared between usernames even if they are on the same account.
The Exchanges Charge for the Entire Calendar Month, even if enabled for only 1 day (No Prorated Fees)
On the Last Day of each calendar month, trading accounts with less than USD 100 (or non-USD equivalent) in equity will have their market data turned off. In the event market data is turned off, the account equity must be increased to USD 100 (or non-USD equivalent) before market data is re-enabled.
The fees above will be the exact amount charged on your AMP Client Statement. These fees may exceed the costs AMP pays to the relevant exchange or datafeed. If there is no price listed, means this exchange is not currently available with the specific datafeed. TT only offers (Level 2) Depth of Market.
