Trusted by a variety of advanced companies & platforms
TradeStation’s trading APIs are built to facilitate your trading ideas, no matter your style or strategy.*
TradingView: Known for its interactive charting capabilities and engaging online trading community, TradingView utilizes TradeStation’s API so its users can trade stocks, ETFs, and futures all within their TradingView dashboard.
Option Alpha: Enables traders to automate their trades, allowing its users to backtest, create, and execute stock and options strategies without advanced coding.
MultiCharts: Combining MultiCharts’ advanced analytics and flexible tools with full access to TradeStation’s market data and trusted trading services, this solution delivers powerful insights and reliable execution in one platform.
QuantConnect: Integrates with TradeStation’s API to help power your algorithmic trading research. Access tools for quantitative analysis, backtesting, and live trading through a unified platform.
TradersPost: Leverages TradeStation’s API to provide access to automated trading strategies across multiple asset classes.
ORATS: Integrates with TradeStation’s API to provide options traders with a centralized platform where they can access their entire portfolio, backtest strategies, launch option scans, and execute trades.
