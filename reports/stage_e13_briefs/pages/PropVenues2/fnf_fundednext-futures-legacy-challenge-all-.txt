 To pass the 
Legacy Challenge
, you must reach the 
profit target
 for your account size while following the 
40% Consistency Rule
 (daily profit can't exceed 40% of your total profit target). There is 
no daily loss limit
, but the 
Maximum Loss Limit
 always applies — 
1,000(25K)
, 
2,000(50K)

3,000(100K)
 — on a Trailing End-of-Day basis. You must follow all prohibited-strategy and micro-scalping rules, and there is 
no time limit
 to pass. 
What is the FundedNext Futures Legacy Challenge?
The Legacy Challenge is a 
1-Step Challenge
 built for traders who prefer 
structure, discipline, and higher reward potential
. It evaluates your ability to earn consistent profits while managing risk in a simulated futures environment during the 
Challenge Phase
. Once you pass, you progress to a 
Legacy FundedNext Account
 where you can withdraw 
Performance Rewards
.

Is there a Consistency Rule in the Legacy Challenge?
Yes.
 The Consistency Rule applies throughout the 
Challenge Phase
. Your profit on any single day cannot exceed 
40% of your total profit target
.
Formula:
 40% × Profit Target = Daily Profit Threshold
If your profit on any single day exceeds this threshold, the total profit required to pass is 
recalculated
:
Required Total Profit = Highest Daily Profit ÷ 40%
Example — $50,000 Legacy Challenge (Profit Target: $3,000)
Daily threshold:
 40% × $3,000 = 
$1,200
 — keep daily profit below this.
If you earn $1,500 in a day:
 $1,500 ÷ 0.40 = 
$3,750
. You would now need 
$3,750
 total to pass, instead of $3,000.
The Consistency Rule encourages steady, repeatable profitability rather than relying on a single high-profit day.

What is the Maximum Loss Limit in the Legacy Challenge?
The 
Maximum Loss Limit (MLL)
 is the total amount you can lose before your account is 
permanently breached
. It follows a 
Trailing End-of-Day (EOD)
 system, updating once per day based on your highest balance that day.

The MLL adjusts at the 
end of each trading day
, not in real time.
Once the MLL reaches your 
initial balance
, it stops trailing and does not move further.
If your 
floating loss
 breaches the MLL during a live trade, your account is breached 
instantly
.

Is there a daily loss limit in the Legacy Challenge?
No.
 The Legacy Challenge has 
no daily loss limit
 at any stage, giving you more flexibility in trade management. The Maximum Loss Limit remains your primary risk guardrail.
What trading rules must I follow in the Legacy Challenge?
You must follow all 
prohibited strategy rules
 throughout the Legacy Challenge to maintain disciplined and ethical trading. Breaking any of these can result in a breach.

What is the Micro-Scalping policy for the Legacy Challenge Account?
Micro-scalping
 is opening and closing trades within 
10 seconds
 to capture small, rapid price movements. Scalping is valid, but a high concentration of these trades affects trading consistency, so FundedNext Futures monitors a 
Micro-Scalping threshold
 across all account types.
During the 
Challenge Phase
:
The Micro-Scalping percentage is the share of your 
total recorded profit
 from trades closed within 10 seconds, evaluated 
per cycle
.
Only profitable trades count
 — you can close losing trades any time without affecting this rule.
At 
30%
, you receive a 
compliance warning
 (no penalty).
At 
40% or above
, your ability to reach the profit target and advance is placed 
on hold
; it resumes 
automatically
 once your percentage drops back below 40%.
🔗

Is there a time limit to pass the Legacy Challenge?
No.
 There is 
no time limit
 — take as long as you need to reach your profit target while following the rules.
What happens after I pass the Legacy Challenge?
Once you meet the profit target while satisfying the Consistency Rule and all trading rules, you move to a 
Legacy FundedNext Account
, where you can withdraw Performance Rewards.
