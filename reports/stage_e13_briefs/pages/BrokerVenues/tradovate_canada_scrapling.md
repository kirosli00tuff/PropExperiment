Opening a Tradovate Account in Canada
-------------------------------------

This article provides our current policy for opening an account as a Canadian resident

* Nov 19, 2024
* Knowledge

### Information

Question

Canadian residents living in the province of Ontario are able to open an account with Tradovate. If you reside in Ontario, [click here to start your account application](https://trader.tradovate.com/register).

Canadian law restricts NinjaTrader from opening accounts for residents of Canada that are outside of the province of Ontario. This includes all other provinces: Quebec, Alberta, Manitoba, British Columbia, Saskatchewan, New Brunswick, Newfoundland, Nunavut, Yukon, Prince Edward Island, Newfoundland, Nova Scotia, and Northwest Territory.

If you are in Canada, but outside of Ontario, you are still able to use Tradovate in Simulated Mode.
