## Method

1. Quote check: every double-quoted string of 12+ characters in each reviewed file is extracted and searched in every
   saved source text under pages/<ROOT>/ (the .txt and .raw.txt pdftotext outputs and the .html.txt page dumps) after
   NFKC normalization (ligatures), dash and quote unification, lower-casing, whitespace collapse, and removal of
   line-break hyphenation. Quotes are split at ellipses and bracketed editorial insertions; a quote matches when every
   fragment of 10+ characters is found. Misses are then inspected by hand (many quoted strings are the readers' own
   hypothesis wordings, not source quotes).
2. Equations and derivations: re-derived by hand and with sympy/numpy in REV_G5_derivations.py; every printed number
   is labelled with the file:line it checks.
3. Worked examples: recomputed from the inputs stated in the text; the readers' own example scripts were run
   read-only where useful.
4. Verdicts: each section's recommendation is compared with the evidence that section cites.
