Contract Rollover
Contract Rollover
TopstepX auto-updates every futures product to the current front month — no need to track rollover dates or volume yourself.
Contracts are automatically rolled over during the daily market close window (4:00 PM – 5:00 PM CST) based on an internal algorithm to determine the optimal time to roll active contracts prior to the next trading day based on volume, open interest, and expiration dates.

Trade Copier
Trade Copier
The Trade Copier lets you mirror trades from a Lead account to 1 or more Follower accounts automatically — so you can run the same strategy across multiple accounts without placing every order manually.
Lead account
— where you place your trades
Follower account(s)
— where those trades are copied to
✋ Before you connect accounts, please know:
The Lead account must have the lowest Maximum Position Size (MPS) of all accounts in the group. Running a 50K, 100K, and 150K? The 50K is your Lead.
If your Lead and Follower accounts are at different
Scaling Plan
tiers — meaning their margin requirements differ — the Trade Copier may disconnect automatically at the start of a new trading day. Always verify your copy trading settings at the start of each session.
Trade copying is only available for Trading Combine® and Express Funded Account® accounts. Live Funded Account® accounts cannot use the Trade Copier.
Filled orders on Follower accounts only appear in the Order Log after they're filled — not when placed.
While a Follower account is connected, you can't trade it directly. If the order buttons are greyed out, the account is still linked. Remove it in Settings to trade it independently.
Executions are mirrored — but exact fill price and timing may vary slightly due to slippage, liquidity, and market conditions.
Turning off the Trade Copier mid-trade will immediately flatten all Follower accounts.
Linking / Unlinking
If the Lead hits its Daily Loss Limit or is auto-liquidated, all Follower accounts are flattened with it and no further trades copy while the Lead is locked out. The accounts stay linked — copying resumes when the Lead can trade again. You may unlink the accounts manually to trade the followers separately.
If the Lead hits its Maximum Loss Limit, the account is permanently violated; unlink the Followers in Settings to trade them independently.
If you select
Liquidate Only
when setting your
PDLL/PDPT
, when of these violations fires on a follower, that account does get dropped from the copier before it's flattened.
If you select
Liquidate and Block
when setting your
PDLL/PDPT
, when one of these violations fires on a follower, the account is locked out. It auto-resumes the next trading and does not get removed from Trade Copier.
If the Scaling plan does not match between the Leader and followers - follower account will be automatically removed at the start of the trading day
When a Payout request is submitted, Follower accounts are automatically unlinked. Check your settings at the start of every session.

Important:
Please read more about how copy trading can affect potential for hedging

Range Bars
Range Bars
👉 Let price — not the clock — decide when a bar prints, so trends, consolidation, and breakouts stand out without the noise of time-based charts.
What it shows
Each Range Bar represents a fixed price move instead of a fixed slice of time. A new bar prints only when price travels your chosen range (say, 10 ticks) — no move, no new bar, no matter how much time passes. Quiet stretches compress into a few bars; strong moves stretch into a long run of them.
How do I add Range Bars?
Range Bars live inside the Advanced Charts (Beta) component. Press the plus sign to add a component, then drag Advanced Charts (Beta) into your layout.
What you'll see
Long run of same-color bars — a real trend, completing bars quickly.
Alternating bars in a tight zone — consolidation. Price isn't committing to a direction.
Bars printing faster — volatility is rising, common around news or the session open.
Bars slowing down — price has stalled.
A clean break from a tight cluster — a shift from balance to directional bars.
Trendlines, channels, and support/resistance tend to line up more consistently than on a time chart.
Where it fits
Range Bars work on any futures contract, on intraday charts. Range size is adjustable per instrument, since a fixed tick count behaves differently across contracts.
👉 Please note:
Range Bars have been launched in Beta as a real-time chart type. Bars build forward from when you open the chart — you can't scroll back to bars that printed before, and reloading or switching symbols starts the build fresh. Historical data (rebuilding past bars from tick history) is planned for a future release.
Range Bars FAQs
​
Why does my data reset?
Beta data resets daily, on top of any reload, symbol switch, or connection drop.
Can I customize my settings?
Yes. At launch you can choose from 4 chart types and 7 ranges (plus a custom range), and you can change bar color, line color, and timezone.
Can I link my chart?
Yes, using the link control in the top-left corner of the component.
Can I add indicators to Advanced Charts?
Not at this time. Because it's a custom chart, a number of things aren't available yet, including indicators, drawing tools, and right-click to place chart orders.
How do I sync the crosshairs from my main chart?
You can't. Crosshair syncing between your main chart and Advanced Charts isn't supported.

Trade Limits
Cap total trades per day and/or week.
Open positions can still be managed

If the Daily Loss Limit value isn't visible in your account header, try these steps:
Set your browser zoom to 100% and make sure the window is fully maximized — header values can disappear when the window is too narrow.
Check for a customization or gear option in the header and confirm "Daily Loss Limit" is enabled.
Confirm you have the correct account selected — the header reflects the active account only.

Orders, stops, or take profits not showing on your chart?
Check chart linking first. Click the link icon on your chart and confirm it's connected to the account you're actively trading. This is the most common reason orders don't appear visually even when they're executing correctly. Performance troubleshooting (data speed, cache) is a separate issue.
Mobile Devices
TopstepX is mobile-friendly and can be accessed through your phone’s web browser, allowing you to manage trades and monitor the market on the go. Most desktop features are available on mobile, providing a consistent and flexible trading experience.
For the best experience, use a smartphone, and tablets may perform better in desktop mode. Please note there is currently no mobile app. If the mobile version is not loading correctly, ensure that “Desktop Mode” is disabled in your browser settings.
Mobile Navigation
Account Info
—View a snapshot of your account balance, UP&L, RP&L, and parameters. Switch between accounts via the dropdown or trigger a Lockout right from this panel — once enabled, it can't be reversed.
All Accounts
—Overview of every account tied to your login, including status (Active, Temp. Liquidation, Goal Achieved, or Ineligible), Created On date, Account Name, Win Rate, Total Lots Traded, Day P&L, Open P&L, and Account Balance.
Positions
—Monitor open positions in with details on Time, Symbol, Position size, Entry Price, Risk (Stop Loss), To Make (Take Profit), P&L, and a 1-tap Close button.
Orders
—Track open, filled, canceled, or rejected orders with key price levels and a quick
Cancel

Trades
—Review your completed round-trip trade history, including Symbol, Size, Time, Entry Price, Exit Price, P&L, and Fees.
