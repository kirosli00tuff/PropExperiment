## Shared rule (prereg reports/stage_e20_prereg_H6.md section 3, verbatim excerpts)

- C: the equity group's close on that trade date: 15:00 CT on a normal day; the early close on a shortened day.
- P(C-30): the close of the bar ending at C-30 (the bar opening at C-31; 14:29 on a normal day).
- P(prior C): the close of the bar ending at the previous equity trade date's C (the bar opening at that date's C-1; 14:59 on a normal day).
- r_ROD = P(C-30) / P(prior C) - 1.
- Entry: market intent on the bar ending at C-30, in sign(r_ROD) (buy if r_ROD > 0, sell if < 0), one MNQ, filling at the next bar's open (C-30).
- Exit: market intent on the first present bar at or after C-2 (opening at or after C-2; 14:58 on a normal day), filling at the next bar's open. F (15:08 CT) and D9.7 are backstops.
- Shortened days: engine F = C-30, so every shortened-day entry is refused (engine_flatten_window).
