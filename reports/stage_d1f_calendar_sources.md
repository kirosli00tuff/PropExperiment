## Systematic gaps (apply across many entries, not itemized above)

- For every MLK Day, Presidents Day, Memorial Day, Labor Day, and Juneteenth entry (2019-2024), CME's own settlement-times PDF confirms the day is not a normal settlement day ("will not derive or disseminate settlement prices") but never states an equity-specific Globex halt clock time. The 12:00 CT halt time for these entries is graded `secondary`, sourced from a generic 2026 broker page (crosstrade.io), not a year-specific source. This mirrors the same gap already flagged in `data/cme_calendar.py`'s docstring for its 2025/2026 entries.
- For 6 of 6 years, no CME-direct settlement-times PDF was found stating the status of the Thanksgiving Day session itself (the fetched PDF titled 'Thanksgiving Day (M/DD/YYYY)' covers only the day AFTER Thanksgiving), **except** 2022 and 2023, whose PDFs happened to include a direct closing note for the Thursday itself. 2019, 2020, 2021, and 2024's Thanksgiving Day entries rely on a generic secondary source.
- No New Year's Eve early close was found in any year 2019-2024 (checked explicitly for the Dec 29, 2023 → Jan 1, 2024 cycle); this differs from the 2025/2026 entries in `data/cme_calendar.py`, which do carry an inferred 12:15 CT New Year's Eve close.
- No national day of mourning or other one-off closure was found for equity index futures anywhere in 2019-2024 (the next one found in any source is January 9, 2025, already covered by `data/cme_calendar.py`).
- New Year's Day 2021 was not individually fetched this session (budget); it is assumed, not confirmed, to follow the same full-closure pattern seen in every other fetched year.
- New Year's Day 2022: no weekday closure was found. January 1, 2022 fell on a Saturday, and CME's own advisory for the following Monday (January 3, 2022) shows NORMAL processing, not a closure. Flagged for the lead to confirm against bar data before assuming a holiday entry is needed for that date.
- The equity early-close clock time varies across years in the raw CME documents even for the same holiday: some years give a single 12:00 CT, others a 15:00→12:00 CT / 15:15→12:15 CT conditional split (2019, 2020 Christmas Eve), and 2024 Christmas Eve gives a plain 12:00 CT — differing from the *inferred* 12:15 CT used for 2025/2026 Christmas Eve in `data/cme_calendar.py`. This is a judgment call for the lead, not resolved here.
- 2021 Good Friday (jobs-report abbreviated session, April 2): CME-direct status confirmed, but no verified close clock time was found (AMP Futures' 2021 page carries the time only as an image, not as page text).
