# Stage E.11 prompt (revision 2026-10-03): research findings F1-F13 and the Task 1 section list, verbatim

F1 The MLL binds, not the edge alone. On a 50K XFA, $85 a day at a 10% ruin probability needs a net Sharpe near 3.5. A realistic successful system runs at net Sharpe 0.8 to 1.8, about $1.5K to $3.5K a month across 5 x 150K. The design reports economics at net Sharpe 1.0, 1.5 and 2.0 as well as against eps, and sets success criteria a realistic system can meet (target band 1.5 to 2.5), never a bar only HFT clears.
F2 Gate 0, a pre-cost component audit, comes first. Combining components that carry no gross edge yields nothing after costs. Before any net model is fit, the audit measures each signal's and the pooled combination's gross (pre-cost) out-of-fold predictive power on the training window. If Gate 0 fails, v2 stops: no net modelling, no phase-2 purchase.
F3 Models: ridge (or elastic net) is the primary model. Shallow LightGBM (depth at most 3 or 4, strong regularization) is the only challenger. No LSTM, transformer or other deep sequence model. ml_route/lstm.py is not reused.
F4 Validation: nested combinatorial purged cross-validation (CPCV) with purge and embargo on the pooled training window, PBO by CSCV, DSR at the full trial count (program N plus every v2 configuration and every Gate 0 test), and t >= 3 on the final configuration.
F5 Statistical power: t is about Sharpe x sqrt(years). A 14-month window cannot confirm a Sharpe near 1.5. Selection uses the pooled training history. The design states the minimum detectable Sharpe for the phase-1 data length and for the full 2019-2024 set.
F6 Horizon and turnover: holds of 60 minutes or longer, 1 to 3 fixed decision times per product per session, exits at fixed horizons or the flatten. A per-product cost-to-volatility (c/sigma) filter, with the threshold stated in the draft and sigma measured on the training window, removes products the cost wall rules out.
F7 Cost gate: trade only when the predicted net edge exceeds k times the round-trip cost, k on the fixed grid {1.5, 2, 3}, chosen by nested CPCV.
F8 Sizing by drawdown distance: D is the account's current distance to the trailing MLL. Target daily P&L sigma about 0.10 x D, maximum loss on any one trade at most 0.25 x D, and size down after each payout.
F9 Payout mechanics are simulated, not assumed: Standard (5 winning days of $150 or more, the cap at 50% of balance up to $2K/$3K/$5K, doubled with the DLL add-on) and Consistency (3 days, largest day at most 40% of the total), the MLL path, resets, and ruin probability, from reports/stage_e0_topstep_facts.json.
F10 Kill switches: a daily loss stop, a drawdown-distance stop, a consecutive-loss stop, a live-versus-expected drift stop and a stale data stop, each with a stated threshold.
F11 Five copied accounts are one bet. Their outcomes are evaluated as one correlated draw, never as five independent ones.
F12 Topstep's Live Funded Account prohibits API automation (research finding). The bot runs on XFAs only. The design records what happens on promotion to Live Funded as a deployment risk in V2.12.
F13 Data: no tick or order-book data. One-minute bars suffice at these horizons.
