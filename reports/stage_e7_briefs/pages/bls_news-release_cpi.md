Consumer Price Index Archived News Releases
===========================================

[Archived News Releases](/bls/news-release/home.htm) | Consumer Price Index
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Consumer Price Index (CPI) news release presents data from the [Consumer Price Indexes](/cpi/) program.

#### Caution

Data in archived news releases may have been revised in subsequent releases. The latest data, including any revisions, may be obtained from the databases accessible on the program homepage: [Consumer Price Indexes: databases](/cpi/#data).
