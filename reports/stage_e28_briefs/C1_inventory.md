## 4. What the program never had

Every signal the program tested was computed from 1-minute OHLCV bars (continuous v.0 series from Databento
GLBX.MDP3), resampled at most to 5-60 minutes or rolled up to daily marks and monthly rebalances. No trade print,
order-book update, queue position or own fill was ever used as an input to a signal, label or model: "F13 no tick or
order-book data | V2.1: one-minute OHLCV only, in every phase" (E.11_RETURN.md:298), and V22 chose "more history, not
finer data (no tick or order-book purchases" (docs/DECISIONS.md:394-395). The program owns order-book data only as
cost-calibration samples: mbp-1 top of book on five fixed dates per contract (E.1_RETURN.md:105) and two MES mbp-10
and trades days that sit inside the sealed holdout-1 (sim/fill_model.py:6-18; progress.md:669); tbbo, mbo, the
statistics schema and second-level bars were never bought, and the 181 MBO days of D.1g were not bought
(docs/DECISIONS.md:30-55). Its limit-order fills are a trade-through rule on minute bars with no queue, volume or
latency model (sim/fill_model.py:50-63), and its market-order cost is a book-walk or half-spread lower bound that
excludes latency and adverse selection (sim/fill_model.py:9-18). The program has never measured its own order
latency or any real or paper fill on TopstepX or another venue. So the mathematics for continuous-time order flow,
point processes, queue and fill-probability models, optimal market making and execution, filtering and LOB deep
learning (section 2) had no data to run on: every one of those methods needs event-time book or trade data that the
program never had.
