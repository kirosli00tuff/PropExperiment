> consolidation: Transition of Master-level Accounts
> consolidation: h
> consolidation: Master-level Account Review
> consolidation: p
> consolidation: When a Trader becomes eligible for transition, Bulenox reviews all of active Master-level Accounts individually. Master-level Account with 3 successful Rewards — eligible for transition to a Funded Account, subject to Risk Management approval. Additional active Master-level Account with at least 1 successful Reward — may also be eligible to transition to a separate Funded Account, subject to Risk Management approval. Active Master Account with 0 successful Rewards — not eligible to transition to a Funded Account. Pending Master-level Account — remains pending and cannot be activated while Trader maintains an active Funded Account. This reflects the current Funded Account Agreement, which specifically permits other active Master-level Accounts with at least one successful Reward to be considered alongside the account that reached three Rewards. Each eligible Master-level Account transitions into its own separate Funded Account.
> consolidation: label
> consolidation: EXAMPLE
> consolidation: p
> consolidation: If five eligible Master Accounts are approved for transition, the Trader may receive five separate Funded Accounts: 5 Master-level Accounts = 5 Funded Accounts. Profits, balance, equity or other monetary amounts accumulated in your Master-level Account do not transfer to the Funded Account. Instead, each Funded Account receives its own Initial End-of-Day (EOD) Drawdown, which establishes the initial trading risk available on that account.

< option-2: Option 2 — EOD Account
< option-2: callout
< option-2: Option 2 uses an end-of-day drawdown, and adds a dynamic scaling plan and a daily loss limit.
< option-2: ✅
< option-2: info
< option-2: h
< option-2: End-of-Day Drawdown
< option-2: p
< option-2: The End-of-Day (EOD) drawdown is updated once per trading day, after the market closes.
< option-2: p
< option-2: Unlike a trailing drawdown, it is based only on your closing account balance. Intraday fluctuations do not affect your threshold.
< option-2: p
< option-2: If your account closes at a new end-of-day high, the drawdown threshold moves up. If it closes below the previous high, the threshold stays where it is.
< option-2: label
< option-2: Example
< option-2: p
< option-2: A trader opens a $100,000 account with a 12-contract limit and a $3,000 EOD drawdown.
