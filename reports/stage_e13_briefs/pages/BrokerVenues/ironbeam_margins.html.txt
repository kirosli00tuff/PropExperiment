Margins Information
Day margins are in effect during all market hours except the last 15 minutes preceding the market close. The margins displayed below are effective for traders using the Ironbeam platform and most supported third-party platforms. Margins are subject to change at any time without prior notice. Exchange/overnight margins fluctuate daily, and you can view real-time margins in the Ironbeam trading platform.
