Do you Allow Canadian Futures Traders to open Accounts?

We accept all Non-Solicited Self-Directed Canadian futures traders when you Open an Optimus Futures account that is cleared through Phillip Capital and Ironbeam.

Please note: Accounts cleared through STONEX, ADMIS, or Wedbush are not available to Canadians.
