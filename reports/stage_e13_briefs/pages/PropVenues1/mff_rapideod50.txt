1. Sim Funded Account Parameters
How the Max Loss works
Your
Max Loss
trails upward each time your account closes at a new end-of-day high.
It does not move intraday – only at the close of each trading session.
The distance between your account and the Max Loss Limit is always
$2,000
while it's trailing.
It never moves down.
​
Max Loss Lock at $100
Once your trailing Max Loss reaches
$100
, it
locks there
.
From that point on, it
does not trail anymore
.
You must
always keep at least $100
in the account.
If your balance goes below $100 → the account is
breached
.
​
News trading
No T1 news trading
is not allowed on this Sim Funded account.
​
Contract limits
You may trade up to
3 minis and/or 30 micros
total.
Exceeding a total of 3 minis or its equivalent in micros (30 micros) can result in a breach of the trading account.

Frequently Asked Question:
Q. Are there any consistency rules in the Rapid EOD evaluation?
A. Yes, there is a 30% consistency rule in the evaluation phase of the Rapid EOD Plan, and it can be passed in as little as 4 days.
Q. Is there a Daily Loss Limit (DLL) on the Rapid EOD Plan?
A. No, there is no daily loss limit in the evaluation or sim funded stages of the Rapid EOD Plan.
Q. How is the drawdown different from the standard Rapid Plan?
A. The standard Rapid Plan trails intraday at the sim-funded stage. Rapid EOD trails end-of-day instead – your Max Loss only moves based on where your account closes each session, not on intraday swings.
Q. Are there any buffer zones in the Rapid EOD payout policy?
A. Yes, you cannot request your first payout until you clear the $2,100 buffer. After that, each additional payout unlocks once you've made $500 in net profit since your last payout.
Q. What's the minimum payout per request on the Rapid EOD Plan?
A. The minimum payout amount on the Rapid EOD Plan is $500.
Q. Are there any activation fees on the Rapid EOD Plan?
A. Our Rapid EOD account does not have an activation fee.
Q. How do I transition to a Live account on Rapid EOD?
A. See
Understanding Rapid Live
for transition triggers, Live account parameters, and the Reserve Program. Rapid EOD 50k follows the same rules as standard Rapid 50k.
Q. Can I trade on live and sim at the same time?
A. No, once you have a live account, you cannot trade on the sim and live environment simultaneously.
