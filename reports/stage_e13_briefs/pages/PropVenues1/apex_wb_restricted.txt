Can Canadians Trade With Apex?
Traders from over 100 countries can participate in the services offered by Apex Trader Funding!
Unfortunately, there are specific reasons why Apex Trader Funding cannot offer trading services in certain banned countries. These reasons include, but are not limited to:
Sanctions or Embargoes:
We comply with international laws restricting financial services to certain countries due to sanctions or embargoes.
Service Limitations by Providers:
Certain brokers, trading platforms, or data providers may be unable to operate in specific regions. We cannot provide full access to live trading accounts without their services.
Increased Fraud Risk:
Countries with higher levels of fraud, such as credit card fraud, identity theft, or online scams, pose significant challenges for secure trading. This also applies to countries with foreign credit card spending restrictions, which can disrupt recurring payments and compliance standards for account ownership.
Language and Training Barriers:
Apex strives to offer high-quality education and support. In some countries, language differences or lack of region-specific support can hinder our ability to provide the desired service level we aim for.
Possible Exceptions for Traders from Restricted Countries:
If you are originally from a restricted country but currently reside and work in a non-restricted country, you may still be eligible to trade with Apex Trader Funding. To qualify, you must meet the following criteria:
You must possess a valid government-issued ID from an
approved (non-restricted) country
.
Your
primary residence, mailing address, and bank account
must be based in a
non-restricted country.
The use or attempted use of any payment method issued from a restricted country is prohibited and constitutes grounds for immediate account termination. This applies to both successful and declined transactions, regardless of the country of residence listed on your account, and remains enforceable at every stage of your account’s lifecycle — including after approval and during active trading.
Please note that while you are in a restricted country, you will not be able to (even once documents are provided):
Purchase new accounts
Request payouts
Place trades on your active accounts.
Maintain full access to your account until you return to a non-restricted country
These restrictions are automatically enforced, and no exceptions are permitted. This policy ensures compliance and security for all users. If you have any questions or need assistance, please submit a HelpDesk ticket

verified during the registration process
to ensure compliance with regulatory requirements. If you meet all of the above conditions, you are welcome to participate in our programs.
