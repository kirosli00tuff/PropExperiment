What Trading Platforms Does FundedNext Futures Offer?
Updated this week
Table of contents
TL;DR:
 FundedNext Futures supports 
three trading platforms
 — 
Tradovate
, 
NinjaTrader
, and 
TradingView
. Each connects directly to your FundedNext Futures Account, so you can trade on the platform that best matches your style, whether you prefer a browser-based setup, advanced desktop tools, or powerful charting.
At FundedNext Futures, we know every trader works differently. That's why you can access your Challenge and FundedNext Account through any of our three supported platforms. Here's a quick look at each so you can choose the right fit.
Tradovate
Tradovate
 is a modern, 
cloud-based futures trading platform
 that runs directly in your browser — no download or installation required. Because it's web-based, you can log in and trade from almost any device, and your setup stays in sync wherever you go. It's a great choice for traders who want a fast, flexible, and low-friction way to get started. 
🔗 
Learn more about trading on Tradovate.
NinjaTrader
NinjaTrader
 is a feature-rich 
desktop trading platform
 built for traders who want advanced charting, order management, and analysis tools. It offers deep customization, extensive technical indicators, and precise order execution, making it a popular choice for active and experienced futures traders who want maximum control over their trading environment. 
🔗 
Learn more about trading on NinjaTrader
TradingView
TradingView
 is one of the world's most popular 
charting and analysis platforms
, known for its clean interface, powerful charting tools, and huge library of indicators. If you already rely on TradingView for market analysis, you can trade your FundedNext Futures account directly from the same platform — combining your favorite charts with live execution in one place. 
🔗 
Learn more about trading on TradingView
