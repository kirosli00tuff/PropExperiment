Trading Combine Subscriptions
Auto-renews monthly and banks a Reset Credit with every Rebill. Here's what you need to know.
September 4, 2026
Table of contents
The Basics
The
Trading Combine®
is a monthly subscription. It Rebills every 30 days from your original sign-up date. Active until you pass and earn an Express Funded Account® (XFA) — or until you cancel. No time limit for passing.
Check your Rebill date and manage your subscription from your
Topstep Dashboard
.
Trading Combine Subscription FAQs
What does the Trading Combine cost?
Topstep Pricing and Payment Information →
What payment methods are accepted?
Credit or debit card:
Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover.
👉 Must be in your own name — using someone else's card violates Topstep's Terms of Use.
Important:
Prepaid cards and gift cards (including Cash App and Venmo) may not work.
How do I cancel?
Billing page →
click "
X
" next to the subscription →
confirm
.
Once canceled, it cannot be undone. No resubscribe option. You also can't purchase a Reset on that subscription after it's canceled.
What happens if I break a rule (hit the Maximum Loss Limit)?
Your account becomes ineligible for funding until it's reset. The subscription does
not
cancel — it keeps rebilling, and you can still trade for practice. You'll get a Reset Credit when your Rebill processes.
What happens if my subscription renewal payment fails?
If a renewal payment fails, your subscription enters a retry period. The system will automatically attempt to process the charge two more times before the subscription is canceled. If you add a new card due to a failed renewal, please remember to update the affected subscription with your new card under the
Subscriptions
section of your Billing tab.
​
During this retry period, account Resets are temporarily disabled for that subscription. Resets will become available again as soon as your subscription payment successfully processes.
Important:
Subscription renewals process early on your rebill date. To avoid unexpected charges or losing the ability to Reset before your next billing cycle,
we recommend completing any account Resets the day before your billing date.
Can I pause my Trading Combine?
No. Subscriptions can't be paused or put on hold. It will Rebill monthly until you pass or cancel.
When do I need to pass to avoid being Rebilled?
Your Dashboard must show you've passed before your Rebill date.
Example:
Rebill is the 5th → pass before trading ends on the 4th.
What happens to my subscription after I pass?
The subscription tied to the passed Trading Combine will auto-cancel, and the account will close. You'll be able to activate your Express Funded Account immediately. Other active Trading Combines keep Rebilling until you manually cancel them.
I passed, but I'm still being charged, what should I do?
Check your Billing page for any other active Trading Combine subscriptions that haven't been passed yet. These will continue to rebill automatically until you cancel them manually. If you believe you're being charged in error for the passed account specifically, please contact support.
Why did I receive a cancellation email after I passed?
When your Trading Combine is marked as Passed, the subscription for that account is automatically canceled. The cancellation email you receive is a standard notification confirming this — it does not indicate any problem with your account or your progress toward funding.
What happens to my Reset Credits if I cancel?
Reset Credits stay on your profile. Available for any future Trading Combine (of the same size/type). However, once a subscription is canceled, you can't Reset that specific account anymore.
Can I reactivate a canceled subscription?
No. Once canceled, it's gone. Start a new Trading Combine to keep trading.
Can I have more than one Trading Combine at a time?
Yes — no limit on how many you can have at once.
