Subscription-Based Fee
Evaluation accounts require a recurring subscription fee while active. Once you pass and move to a funded account, your subscription ends automatically.
There are 
no activation fees
 and 
no recurring subscription fees
 on funded accounts.
Reset Fees
If a user fails their 
evaluation account or funded simulated account
, they are eligible to purchase a reset which will put their account back to their original starting parameters. Users are allowed 
unlimited
 resets for 
evaluation accounts. 
On 
funded accounts
, users are allowed up to 
three
 resets max. (Not available on S2F Accounts)
One Time Fee
Only account offered with a one time purchase fee is our S2F account. This account will allow you to bypass the evaluation phase and quickly enter the funded simulated stage.
