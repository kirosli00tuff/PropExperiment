Does FundedNext Futures allow overnight and weekend trade holding?

June 10, 2026
Table of contents

TL;DR: FundedNext Futures does not allow overnight or weekend trade holding. Traders must close all positions by 3:10 PM Central Time (CT) each trading day. Positions not closed by this time are automatically closed. Trading can resume at 5:00 PM CT, and after the weekend, trading resumes on Sunday at 5:00 PM CT.

Overnight and weekend holdings are not permitted at FundedNext Futures. It is important to remind traders that they must close their positions by 3:10 PM Central Time every day. If they fail to do so, their positions will be automatically closed in order to mitigate risk and safeguard their accounts. Trading can then resume at 5:00 PM Central Time.

We require that our traders close their positions daily before 3:10:00 PM CT, Monday through Friday. You can resume trading at 5:00 PM Central Time. After the weekend, trading resumes on Sunday at 5:00 PM CT.

What are the overnight trading policies?

  • Not Permitted: Overnight holding of positions is strictly prohibited.

  • Trading Day End Time:

    • 3:10 PM Central Time (CT)

  • System Auto-Close: If you fail to close trades on your own, the system will automatically close all open positions at the end of the trading day.

Why is this policy in place for FundedNext Futures?

  • Risk Management: Overnight holding or weekend holding exposes traders to potential gap risks when the market reopens.

  • Account Protection: Automatic liquidation prevents traders from incurring unintended losses due to price fluctuations outside trading hours.

  • Stable Trading Environment: Ensures all traders operate under the same risk-controlled conditions.

Trader's Responsibility: It is the trader’s responsibility to monitor their positions and ensure they are closed before the designated market closes each day.
