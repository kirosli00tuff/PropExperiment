CME Exchange Fee Increase (Effective February 1, 2025)
By:
AMP Futures
on
Jan 31, 2025, 2:03:35 PM
CME
Important Trading Information
Effective February 1, 2025, Chicago Mercantile Exchange Inc. (“CME”), The Board of Trade of the City of Chicago, Inc. (“CBOT”), New York Mercantile Exchange, Inc. (“NYMEX”), and Commodity Exchange, Inc. (“COMEX”) (collectively, the “Exchanges”) will adjust transaction fees as described in the tables below.
We have updated the AMP Exact Cost Calculator - CLICK HERE TO VIEW (All-In) PRICING >>
NO CHANGES to Transaction (Exchange) fees for CME/CBOT EQUITY PRODUCTS :) !!!!
Contracts / Symbols ( ES, YM, NQ, RTY, MES, MNQ, MYM, M2K ) - all other symbols remain the same.
Here are the specific changes below - will be adjusted as follows effective February 1, 2025
Transaction (Exchange) fees for CBOT Agricultural Futures ( Corn, Wheat, Soybeans, Soybean Oil, Soybean Meal, Oats, Rough Rice )
Per contract - Exchange Fee > Changes from $2.10 to $2.13
Transaction (Exchange) fees for CBOT Agricultural Mini Futures ( Corn, Wheat, Soybeans, Soybean Oil, Soybean Meal, Oats, Rough Rice )
Per contract - Exchange Fee > Changes from $1.03 to $1.06
Transaction (Exchange) fees for CBOT Agricultural Options ( Corn, Wheat, Soybeans, Soybean Oil, Soybean Meal, Oats, Rough Rice )
Per contract - Exchange Fee > Changes from $2.10 to $2.14
Transaction (Exchange) fees for COMEX / NYMEX METALS Options ( Gold, Silver, Copper, Platinum )
Per contract - Exchange Fee > Changes from $1.60 to $1.65
Transaction (Exchange) fees for COMEX Micro Gold Futures
Per contract - Exchange Fee > Changes from $0.50 to $0.60
